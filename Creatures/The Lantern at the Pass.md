## What the PCs Know

The god of [[Saint Aubren's Rest]]: a god of travellers and of shelter in winter, whose
symbol is a lit lamp. Its carvings are on the wayside shrine.

## Encounters

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Its whole lamp is carved on the wayside shrine, matching Ilyra's rubbing; a silver votive lamp of it was in the offering niche.

## GM Notes

### Behind the Scenes

The order's original god, which the bargain betrays. Its carvings have been
chiselled off inside the monastery, except the symbol over the abbot's door,
which nobody recognises any more. Its last cleric is Lucan (Life Domain).
