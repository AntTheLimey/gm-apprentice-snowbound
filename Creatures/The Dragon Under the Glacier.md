## What the PCs Know

The party has seen it: a white dragon asleep on forty winters of frozen coin in a cavern of blue ice under the glacier, with pilgrims standing in the walls. It woke as they came down and talked before it fought. It calls itself the first guest of the house, there before the saint and his lamp; the saint called it "Guest". It says the order's sickness is its own breath, which it gives and takes back for the dead. It offered each of them something, named its price (Lucan, the lamp put out, and one of them to keep its house), and when they refused said it would have Long Night early.

## Encounters

- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Woke in its cavern as the party came down: called itself the first guest of the house, said the sickness is its breath, offered each of them something, and named its price, Lucan. Refused, it said "Then I will have Long Night early."
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Fought the party on its coin: dropped Lucan twice, bargained when bleeding and was refused, and carried Lucan under the ice. Kael killed it through the ice. ([[The Death of the Guest]])

## GM Notes

### Stats

SRD **Young White Dragon** (CR 6; AC 17; HP 123; flies, burrows and swims).
Tuned for session 4 below: the party arrives on one short rest, with
Lucan supporting.

**Initiative** +3 (13) (SRD).

**Session 4 balance tuning (GM, 2026-11-18; Ant chose the package).** The SRD Young White Dragon block above stays as published; these are added for the fight only, so one dragon can stand against six PCs and Lucan:

- **Hit Points** 183 (the SRD's 123 + 60).
- ***Legendary Resistance (2/Day).*** When it fails a saving throw, it can succeed instead.
- ***Legendary Actions (3).*** Immediately after another creature's turn, it can spend one use; it regains all uses at the start of its turn.
  - ***Rend (1 use).*** One Rend attack.
  - ***Ice Walk (1 use).*** It moves up to half its Speed across the ice without provoking Opportunity Attacks.
  - ***Wing Buffet (2 uses).*** Each creature within 10 ft makes a Dexterity save, DC 15: 9 (2d4 + 4) Bludgeoning damage and Prone on a failure. Then it flies up to half its Fly Speed.
- ***Lair Actions*** (on initiative 20, losing ties; never the same one two rounds running):
  - ***Icefall.*** Icicles fall from the cavern roof on up to three points it can see within 120 ft: each creature within 5 ft of a point makes a Dexterity save, DC 15, or takes 10 (3d6) Piercing damage.
  - ***Freezing Fog.*** Fog rises off the meltwater in a 20-ft-radius sphere it can see until initiative 20 next round: Heavily Obscured (its blindsight 30 ft sees through it).
  - ***Sliding Hoard.*** The cavern floor is its hoard: forty winters of pilgrims' coins, frozen into the ice. A 20-ft square of it cracks loose and tilts: each creature there makes a Dexterity save, DC 15, or falls Prone and slides 10 ft downhill toward the meltwater.

### Behaviour

Sleeps under the glacier. The order gives it their dead, and pilgrims when too
few monks die; in return its breath in the ice cures the order's sickness. It
is fed on Long Night.

**The sickness is its breath** (GM ruling, 2026-11-18). Its cold rises through the ice into the house and sickens the monks; its breath frozen into the ice cures them. It keeps the house sick so the house keeps it fed. It was under the glacier before the house; the Lantern's lamp held its cold back until the order cut the lamp off the walls. The monks call it the Guest; it gives no name. It hates the whole lamp lit.

**If the party tries to Long Rest instead of coming down** (GM ruling, Ant, 2026-11-18): about an hour in (a Short Rest's benefit only), it claws its way up from the glacier through the floor of the chapter house. It opens with a free Cold Breath before initiative, at whoever is nearest. The whole house counts as its lair: its lair actions work there, recast (Icefall off the cloister eaves, Freezing Fog as steam from the broken floor, Sliding Hoard as the flagstones heaving), with its tuned Legendary Resistance and Legendary Actions.

### Signs It's Near

- Steam from a vent in the glacier, seen from the bell tower.
- Ice shards that heal, in the infirmary reliquary.

### Treasure

Forty winters of pilgrims, plus what the dragon had before the order came.

- **Coin:** about 2,400 gp in mixed old coin, frozen into the cave floor (an hour's work with picks to free it all).
- **Gems:** a dozen river-cut garnets and moonstones, about 600 gp the lot.
- **Frost Brand** (longsword; very rare, requires attunement), stuck point-down in the ice where its last owner fell.
- **Boots of the Winterlands** (uncommon, requires attunement), still on a frozen pilgrim's feet.
- **Cloak of Protection** (uncommon, requires attunement), folded in a pilgrim's pack.
- **The pilgrims' belongings:** packs, holy symbols, letters home. Among them, [[Tam Rowe]]'s coat, with Hesta's letter in the pocket (his pack stayed under his bed in the guest wing; GM ruling 2026-11-18).

### Hidden Ties

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** slow, amused, courteous; counts in winters and names; never raises its voice.
- **Tell:** breathes out between sentences, and the cold comes off it in waves you can count.
- **Line:** "I was the first guest of this house."

### Wants

The house kept sick and fed, Lucan given to it, and the whole lamp put out for good.
