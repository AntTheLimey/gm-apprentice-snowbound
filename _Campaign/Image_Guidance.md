# Image Guidance

<!-- gm-only -->

## Direction

Cold outside, warm lamplight inside. The monks look completely sincere and gentle, never sinister: the horror is that they are kind. Show only what the players have seen or been told. Since session 3 they have seen the ice crypt, the ice cave and the dragon: a young white dragon in a cavern of blue ice, lying on a hoard of frozen coins, with frozen pilgrims standing upright in the walls. The glacier stair is still unseen. Gilt is the fat, moustached merchant until the unmasking in session 2; from then he is Lucan, a thin, older man with a grey-shot beard, in oiled mail and a white surcoat with the whole lamp worked in gold on the breast.

## Always

Painterly fantasy book illustration with a gouache texture. Muted winter palette of slate blue, grey and snow white, with warm amber lamplight. Medieval high-mountain setting; nothing modern; no blood or gore.

No signature, watermark or lettering anywhere in the picture.

## Blocks

### Portrait

Head and shoulders, three-quarter view, the face clearly lit and in character, against a soft plain background.

### Place

A wide establishing view at eye level with the weather visible; any people small, only for scale.

### Creature

The whole figure in its setting, mid-action.

### Emblem

A carved or painted symbol on stone or cloth, centred, against a plain background.

### Map

A hand-drawn ink-and-wash map on old parchment, as an explorer would sketch it, clear shapes, no words, numbers or labels.
