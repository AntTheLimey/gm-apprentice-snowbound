# Snowbound

## Premise

Four sellswords are hired in Harrowford to escort Master Orrin Gilt, a fat and
fussy cloth merchant, up the pass to Saint Aubren's Rest, a mountain monastery
where he means to winter in prayer. The monks are the kindest people the party
has ever met. The snow closes the pass behind them. Then the pilgrims start to
disappear.

## Setting

A high mountain pass at the onset of winter, in a generic D&D 5e (2024) world
built on the SRD, homebrewed as needed. Saint Aubren's Rest is cut down into
the mountain: a warm guest wing at the top, older and colder levels below. The
order serves the Lantern at the Pass, a god of travellers and winter shelter.

## Key Themes

- Kindness as a mask: being watched in a house where everyone is warm.
- A refuge that has become a trap.
- Teacher and pupil; the one who stayed and the one who left.

## Current Arc

The abbot is dead and his order broken, and Lucan's accusation stood. The party has gone down under the glacier with Lucan and the whole lamp lit, and the dragon the house fed has woken and named its price. Four sessions: the climb and the first night; the
investigation and the unmasking; the fight with the monks and the abbot; the
descent and the dragon.

## Key Factions

- **[[The Order of Saint Aubren]]:** the monks of the Rest, led by
  [[Abbot Severin]]. Hospitable to every traveller.

## GM Notes

> [!info] GM Only
> The order feeds its dead to [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]] in return for
> its breath in the ice, which cures the sickness the order has carried for
> generations. Too few monks die, so the abbot chooses pilgrims from a roster
> in the guest register; every monk knows. The client is Lucan, a novice who
> ran away twenty years ago and has come back, disguised by a
> [[Hat of Disguise]], as a Life Domain cleric of the Lantern to kill the dragon. The
> abbot was his teacher and saw through the disguise on the first evening.
> Tone: snowbound horror. The unmasking always ends session 2. Party: four
> PCs at level 5, with Lucan as an NPC ally. Test campaign for the #556 table
> screen.
