# Snowbound — Timeline

## Day 1

- **Day 1, morning** — On [[The Pass]] the mule slips on the ice shelf; [[Master Orrin Gilt]]'s chest falls to a ledge and is recovered.
- **Day 1, midday** — Four [[Ice Mephit|ice mephits]] attack at the wayside shrine; the party finds the whole lamp carving and the [[Silver Votive Lamp]].
- **Day 1, dusk** — Arrival at [[Saint Aubren's Rest]]; supper with the monks and pilgrims.
- **Night 1** — Snow closes the pass. [[Brother Teodor]] carries a sack down the inner stair; [[Tam Rowe]] disappears ([[The Disappearance of Tam Rowe]]).

## Day 2

- **Day 2, morning** — Tam's place at breakfast is empty; [[Abbot Severin]] names the dead brother as [[Brother Matthias]].
- **Day 2** — The party splits up: [[Kael Stonehand]] reads the guest register, [[Ilyra Vessant]] climbs the bell tower with [[Durgan Flintmere]], [[Brannoc Hale]] hears [[Aldous Crane]]'s report, and [[Nim Underbough]] lifts [[Master Orrin Gilt]]'s purse.
- **Day 2, dusk** — The office for [[Brother Matthias]]; [[Abbot Severin]] announces a vigil for Long Night "to honour our guest the merchant".
- **Night 2** — [[Brother Teodor]] comes for Gilt, who unmasks as Lucan and accuses the abbot before the guest wing ([[The Unmasking of Lucan]]).
- **Night 2** — The fight in the guest wing corridor: [[Abbot Severin]] and [[Brother Teodor]] die, Lucan falls and lives, and the order yields ([[The Death of Abbot Severin]]).
- **Night 2, after the fight** — [[Kael Stonehand]] finds [[Tam Rowe]]'s boot in the crypt; [[Oswin]] reads his name on the register's Long Night page; [[Grenna Fallowmere]] takes three of [[Sister Wren]]'s ice shards.
- **Night 2, before dawn** — Lucan leads the party down the crypt chute past Tam's body to [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]], which names its price.
- **Night 2, before dawn** — The fight on the coin: [[Kael Stonehand]] kills [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]] through the ice, and Lucan lives ([[The Death of the Guest]]).
- **Night 2, before dawn** — In the hoard [[Kael Stonehand]] finds [[Tam Rowe]]'s coat and [[Hesta's Letter]]; Kael and [[Oswin]] carry Tam up the chute.

## Day 3

- **Day 3, dawn** — [[Oswin]] unbars [[The Chapter House]] and keeps the lamp lit; [[Sister Wren]] chooses a brother for her shards; [[Brother Hollis]] reads the register's names to [[Ilyra Vessant]].

## Late winter and spring

- **Late winter** — [[Kael Stonehand]] and [[Grenna Fallowmere]] go down [[The Pass]] a month early; three shards cure [[Hesta Rowe]].
- **Spring** — The pass opens. [[Brother Hollis]] and an old brother die; the brothers choose [[Sister Wren]]; everyone else walks down.
