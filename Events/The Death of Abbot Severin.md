## What Happened

- The fight in the guest wing corridor opened on "Peace, child. Rest." [[Nim Underbough]] dropped [[Brother Hollis]] with her first dagger.
- [[Oswin]], a novice of the house, would not fight in the first round and stood between the abbot and Lucan: "Father. Is it true?"
- [[Brother Teodor]] fell to [[Kael Stonehand]]'s arrows and blind [[Aldous Crane]]'s sword.
- The abbot's cold dropped Lucan twice; [[Brother Ansel]]'s mace glanced off him as he lay.
- In the second round Oswin turned on his abbot ("No, Father") and beat him down; the abbot never raised a hand to him.
- The rest of the order came up the inner stair and [[Ilyra Vessant]] webbed them on it.
- The abbot died in [[Grenna Fallowmere]]'s moonlight: "Someone must keep the house alive." The brothers stopped.

## Why It Matters

The house's bargain has lost its abbot. The order is broken and barred in the chapter house, and nothing now stands between the party and the dragon under the glacier but the way down.
