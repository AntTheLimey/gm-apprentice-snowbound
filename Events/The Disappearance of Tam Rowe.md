## What Happened

- At supper [[Brother Hollis]] brought [[Tam Rowe]] a cup to help him sleep, and Tam went to bed early.
- After midnight [[Brannoc Hale]] heard dragging and found [[Brother Teodor]] carrying a heavy sack down an inner stair; Teodor said a brother had died, and would not let him past the frosted door at the bottom.
- The snow closed the pass in the night.
- At breakfast Tam's place was empty. Hollis said he had left before dawn. Brannoc asked which brother had died; [[Abbot Severin]] said [[Brother Matthias]].
- [[Kael Stonehand]] found no tracks anywhere, and Tam's pack and Hesta's salve still under his bed.

## Why It Matters

Kael came up the pass to find Tam. Nobody has left the Rest by the path, and the monks' story does not match what Brannoc saw.
