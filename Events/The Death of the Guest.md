## What Happened

- [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]] opened with the roof: icicles fell on the lamp and the people round it.
- [[Brannoc Hale]] walked out across the coin and hit it three times; its breath put Lucan down at its feet, and [[Grenna Fallowmere]] got him up.
- Bleeding, it bargained: spare it and it would breathe for the sick every winter. Grenna did the sum out loud, six shards for four sick, and told [[Kael Stonehand]] she had three for Hesta. Everyone refused.
- Its second breath dropped Lucan again, and it carried him in its jaws under the meltwater ice. Grenna woke him in its mouth.
- Kael killed it through the ice, ten feet short of the glacier wall. Brannoc hauled Lucan out.

## Why It Matters

The house's bargain is over. The dragon's cold has stopped rising, so nobody new sickens; the four already sick have six shards between them, enough for two.
