## What Happened

- On night 2 [[Brother Hollis]] brought warm cups round the guest wing. [[Kael Stonehand]] saw him pour Gilt's from a small jug of its own and knocked it out of Gilt's hand.
- After midnight Gilt shouted "Not tonight, Teodor." [[Brother Teodor]] was in his doorway with a rope and Hollis's lamp, and stepped back.
- Gilt tore off his hat: a thin, older man in oiled mail and a white surcoat with the whole lamp. "I am Lucan. You taught me the hymn, Father."
- Before the pilgrims and [[Abbot Severin]], he accused the house: guests drugged and carried "down to rest", their bodies given to [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]] for the cure in the ice, the register's lamp marks the abbot's list, and [[The Lantern at the Pass]] cut off the walls.
- Kael asked where [[Tam Rowe]] was. "Below. With the others."
- The abbot did not deny it: "Peace, child. Someone must keep the house alive." The brothers closed round him.

## Why It Matters

The merchant the party escorted is the abbot's runaway pupil, back to end the bargain. The house's secret has been said out loud in front of every guest, and the brothers are about to fight.
