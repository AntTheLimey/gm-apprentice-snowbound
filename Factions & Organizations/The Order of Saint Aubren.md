## Overview

The monks of [[Saint Aubren's Rest]]: a hospitable order that keeps the pass open to
travellers in winter, under [[The Lantern at the Pass]]. Led by [[Abbot Severin]].

The house's kindness had a price. The brothers gave their dead, and the guests they drugged, to the dragon under the glacier, and in return its cold breath in the ice cured the sickness they carry. Every brother knew. Since the abbot's death the order is broken, its brothers barred in the chapter house.

## Goals & Methods

Shelter for any traveller caught on the mountain. Hymns, bells, soup.

## Resources

Monks trained in the Open Hand.

## History

Founded by Saint Aubren as a winter refuge.

## GM Notes

### The Bargain

The order gives its dead to the dragon under the glacier; the dragon's breath
in the ice cures the sickness the order has carried for generations (without
it, monks die in spring). Too few monks die, so the abbot chooses pilgrims
from a roster written in the guest register. Everyone in the order knows;
every monk has agreed.
