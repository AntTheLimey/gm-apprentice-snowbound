# Session 2: the open investigation

No clue is required. Each place reveals something and points to at least two
others. The unmasking always closes session 2 (Ant); the routes decide how.

| Place or person | What's there | Points to |
|-----------------|--------------|-----------|
| **The guest register** (Hollis, guest wing) | Arrivals for forty winters. Small lamp marks beside some names, and none of those marked is ever recorded leaving. "Master Orrin Gilt" is marked, this year, first. | The crypt; Hollis; Gilt |
| **The infirmary** (Sister Wren) | Sick brothers with frost-blue lips, recovering. A reliquary of ice shards that heal. Wren's guilt, if pushed. | The crypt (where the ice comes from); the abbot |
| **The bell tower** (the dwarf stonemason) | The old stair down is blocked with newer stone. The old lamp carvings are chiselled off everywhere except over the abbot's door. From the top, steam rises from a vent in the glacier. | The chapter house; the glacier stair |
| **The blind soldier's nights** | Dragging footsteps going down. Footsteps from the merchant's room every hour, all night. | Teodor and the crypt; Gilt |
| **The crypt door** (Brother Teodor) | Sealed, rimed with frost. Inside, the newest niches are empty. Drag marks run to a chute. The young husband's boot. | The glacier; the abbot |
| **The abbot's tests** | The hymn, the lamp oil ("you'll know where"), the changed grace word. Gilt flinches or covers too smoothly. The widow noticed the grace. | Gilt; Severin knows him |
| **The relic-seller** (red herring) | Caught creeping at night: she's prising gold leaf off the chapel lamps. She saw Hollis marking the register by candlelight. | The register |
| **The boy and Ansel** | Ansel is grooming the boy to take vows. He lets slip: "The abbot never looked at me the way he looks at that merchant." | Gilt; Severin |
| **Bumping into Gilt** | A thin man inside a fat one. | Gilt |

**Routes to the unmasking:**

1. The tests, the widow and the soldier: the PCs confront Gilt, and he tells
   them, then asks for their help.
2. The register: why is a stranger marked first? Asking Hollis or Gilt opens it.
3. Fallback (Ant): at the end of session 2, if the PCs haven't gathered enough, Lucan
   reveals himself anyway and accuses the abbot. It always happens.
