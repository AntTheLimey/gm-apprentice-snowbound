It has offered each of them something and named its price: Lucan, the lamp put out, and one of them to keep its house. Everyone refused. It has just said "Then I will have Long Night early." Full HP (tuned: 183), breath charged, Legendary Resistance 2, lair actions live.

**Situation:** The Guest has stopped talking. A white dragon uncoils off its hoard (forty winters of pilgrims' coins, frozen into the cavern floor) in a cavern of blue ice, and it wants Lucan, and the lamp out.

**Starts it:** [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]]: "Then I will have Long Night early." Roll initiative. Positions, hit points and gear are in the Paused section above.

**NPCs**
- **[[The Dragon Under the Glacier]]:** wants Lucan eaten and the whole lamp put out. Opens with Cold Breath on whoever stands round the lamp. After that it goes for Lucan, and then for whoever holds the lamp. Tuned stats, legendary actions and lair actions are under its GM Notes.
- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]] (Lucan):** wants it dead, and can't do it alone. He has one 3rd-level slot (*Spirit Guardians*), one Channel Divinity (*Preserve Life*) and *Healing Word*. He stays by the lamp and sings, and he takes a blow meant for a PC.

**Points to land**
- [ ] It hates the lamp: when the lamp is lifted, it flinches and turns its head away.
- [ ] Bloodied (91 HP or less), it stops and offers a new bargain (see Then).
- [ ] What killing it does to the sick: the cold stops rising, so nobody new sickens. The ones already blue keep only the shards.

**If the players...**

| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Fight it | Lair actions on initiative 20: Icefall, Freezing Fog and Sliding Hoard, never the same one two rounds running. Legendary actions come after other creatures' turns |
| Bring it to 91 HP or less | It stops fighting for a breath and bargains: spare it, and it breathes for the sick (Hesta and the brothers, every winter), takes only the dead who die on their own, and one of them keeps the house. "Kill me, and the ones already blue have six shards between them." It keeps a bargain to the letter |
| Accept its bargain | The fight stops. Lucan won't stop: he raises the lamp and goes for it alone, and the PCs have to hold him back or fight beside him. The dragon keeps its word: the sick live, the house stays fed, and someone has to carry |
| Refuse the bargain | It fights on. At 40 HP or less it tries to burrow down into the glacier floor and get away. *Web*, a grapple or being knocked prone stops it for a turn |
| Press a shard on someone | It heals 2d4 + 3 (an action). Each shard spent is a third of someone's cure. Grenna holds three, and only Kael suspects |
| Work the Frost Brand out of the ice | Strength (Athletics) DC 15, as an action. Without attunement it is a plain longsword for now |
| Lift the lamp toward it | It flinches: the first attack it makes that turn has Disadvantage. Lucan starts the hymn with "light" |
| Run for the slope and the chute | See the contingency The Party Retreats Up the Chute in the Plan |

**Complications**
- Sliding Hoard (the coin floor tilting) throws someone toward the meltwater, under ice too thin to stand on.
- Freezing Fog rises around the lamp, and the light goes blue inside it.
- One of the people standing in the walls is wearing Boots of the Winterlands. Another is a brother of the house, in his habit, with frost-blue lips.

**Treasure**
- About 2,400 gp in old coin frozen into the floor (an hour's work with picks).
- A dozen garnets and moonstones, about 600 gp.
- Frost Brand (longsword; very rare, requires attunement), point-down in the ice.
- Boots of the Winterlands (uncommon, requires attunement), on a frozen pilgrim.
- Cloak of Protection (uncommon, requires attunement), in a pilgrim's pack.
- [[Tam Rowe]]'s coat, with [[Hesta's Letter]] in the pocket.

> The floor moves first. It is coins, thousands of them frozen into the ice, and they crack loose and skitter as the white thing uncoils. The cold comes off it in waves you can count, and the lamp gutters, and holds.
