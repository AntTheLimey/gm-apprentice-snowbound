**Situation:** After the short rest, under the one uncut lamp carving over the abbot's door. Lucan has lit an oil lamp beneath it and is singing the old evening hymn with "light" where the house sang "breath".

**Starts it:** [[Master Orrin Gilt|Lucan]], to the whole corridor: "All of you. Bring what you have. We go down tonight, before Long Night."

**NPCs**
- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]]:** Lucan. Wants the dragon dead tonight, the whole lamp carried down, and the PCs beside him. Left alone, he goes down the inner stair at dawn by himself.
- **[[Marda Vell]]:** wants the hymn sung right, once. Sings it with him, and weeps at the last verse.
- **[[Aldous Crane]]:** wants orders. Holds the guest wing if Brannoc gives him the word.
- **[[Durgan Flintmere]]:** wants to see the old stair. Offers to open it: "an hour's loud work".
- **[[Pip Callow]]:** wants to come. Asks Oswin.

**Points to land**
- [ ] Two ways down: the crypt chute (fast, quiet, past the dead) and the old Glacier Stair behind the bell tower's newer stone (slow and loud).
- [ ] Lucan asks for the whole lamp: Ilyra's silver votive lamp, lit, keeps the cold off whoever stands near it.
- [ ] He cannot do it alone, and says so.

|---|---|

> The abbot's door is shut. Above it the carved lamp is whole, flame and all, and a little oil lamp burns under it on the flags. Lucan is singing. It is the evening hymn, and he sings "light".
