**Situation:** Night 2, after midnight, in the guest wing corridor: Lucan unmasked and armed, the abbot and three brothers closing round him, the pilgrims in their doorways, two newcomers at either end. Session 3 opens on initiative.

**Starts it:** [[Abbot Severin]], gently: "Peace, child. Rest." Roll initiative.

**NPCs**
- **[[Abbot Severin]]:** wants Lucan dead tonight and the house kept. Left alone, he goes for Lucan first, keeps Teodor in front of him, and speaks every name from the register.
- **[[Brother Teodor]]:** wants to carry the sleepers down without fuss. Holds the corridor, never chases, stuns and drags toward the inner stair. Pulls his blows against [[Oswin]].
- **[[Brother Hollis]]:** wants the abbot standing. *Sanctuary* on Severin, *Healing Word* on the fallen; flees to the register if the abbot falls.
- **[[Brother Ansel]]:** wants Lucan, out of jealousy. Goes for him first; drops everything to shield [[Pip Callow]] once Pip arrives.
- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]]:** Lucan, the PCs' ally. Wants to face Severin. Supports: *Bless*, *Warding Bond*, *Healing Word*.
- **Arriving, start of round 3:** [[Sister Wren]] and four Brothers of the Open Hand (stats under [[The Order of Saint Aubren]]) up the inner stair, behind the abbot, at [[Oswin]]'s end. [[Pip Callow]] trails them with a lamp.
