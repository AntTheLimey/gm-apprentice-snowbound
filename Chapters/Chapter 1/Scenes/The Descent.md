**Situation:** Night 2, toward dawn, going down into the glacier by one of two ways, in a cold no house was built for.

**Starts it:** The crypt's frosted door swinging open on cold air, or the last of Durgan's newer stone falling away from the old stair.

**NPCs**
- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]]:** Lucan. Goes first if they let him; sings under his breath.

**Points to land**
- [ ] The cold gets worse with every step: below the house it is Extreme Cold (SRD: Constitution save DC 10 at the end of each hour or 1 Exhaustion level).
- [ ] The whole lamp, lit, keeps it back: creatures within 10 ft of it have Advantage on saves against the cold, the vent's gust included.
- [ ] Every few minutes the sleeping dragon breathes, and the whole glacier breathes with it.

**If the players...**

| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Take the crypt chute | About ten minutes, one cold save. See The chute and The ice cave below |
| Take the Glacier Stair | About an hour after Durgan's hour of work, two cold saves. See The icicle gallery, The vent and The snow bridge below; the dragon is awake when they arrive |
| Go without the lamp lit | No Advantage on the cold saves |
| Rope together | Advantage on saves to stay on their feet on the ice; a creature that falls is held if the next on the rope makes a Strength save DC 12 |

**The chute** (crypt route)
- Eighty feet of ice polished smooth by forty winters of bodies, dropping out of the lamplight.
- Sliding down: Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 13 or 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage, landing Prone among the bones. A rope belayed at the top: no check.

**The ice cave** (crypt route)
- The order's dead, and the pilgrims, laid in rows in the ice. [[Tam Rowe]] lies at the foot of the chute, frozen, in his shirt; his coat is gone.
- A meltwater pool under a skin of thin ice (SRD Thin Ice: holds 3d10 x 10 lb per 10-ft square; below it, Frigid Water) lies between the cave and the slope down to the lair.
- The slope beyond is Slippery Ice (Difficult Terrain; Dexterity save DC 10 or Prone). Coming down it, they see the lair from above while the dragon sleeps.

**The icicle gallery** (stair route)
- The old bell rope is frozen across the steps a hand above the ice, holding up a curtain of great icicles. The monks' guard on the old way.
- Wisdom (Perception) DC 11 spots it; stepping over it is safe once seen. Crossing it unseen: each creature below makes a Dexterity save DC 15, 22 (4d10) Piercing damage on a failure, half on a success.

**The vent** (stair route)
- Every few minutes a gust of frost breathes up the stair from below: Constitution save DC 13, 7 (2d6) Cold damage, and every open flame but the whole lamp goes out.
- Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Nature) DC 12 counts the breaths: they move between them and make no save.

**The snow bridge** (stair route)
- A crevasse thirty feet deep across the stair, bridged by old snow (SRD Thin Ice: 3d10 x 10 lb per 10-ft square). Falling in: 10 (3d6) Bludgeoning damage and a climb out on rope or a DC 15 Strength (Athletics) check.

## GM Notes

- Built from the SRD's Extreme Cold, Slippery Ice, Thin Ice and Frigid Water, and its Collapsing Roof and Hidden Pit traps at levels 5–10, reskinned to ice.
- The whole lamp's warmth is a homebrew ruling for Snowbound (2026-11-18): the Lantern at the Pass is a god of shelter in winter.
- Route choice is the players'. The chute is quick and passes Tam's body; the stair is long and loud and wakes the dragon.
