**Situation:** Dusk, day 2, in the chapel: the whole house and every guest at the office for a brother who never lived.

**Starts it:** The bell; [[Brother Hollis]] at the guest wing door with his lamp: "Come, come. He'd have liked a full chapel."

> The chapel is full of small lamps. The shroud lies on its trestle under the abbot's hand, and the brothers sing the old evening hymn, softly, as if somebody were asleep.

**NPCs**
- **[[Abbot Severin]]:** wants to test Gilt. Hums the old evening hymn behind Gilt's place as the office begins.
- **[[Brother Ansel]]:** sings; watches the abbot watching Gilt.
- **[[Brother Teodor]]:** stands at the back, by the door.
- **[[Marda Vell]]:** sings every word a beat early; says "light" aloud where the abbot says "breath".

**Points to land**
- [ ] Gilt goes still at the hymn.
- [ ] Severin announces the Long Night vigil, the merchant to be honoured.
