The whole of Snowbound: four sessions. It starts on the climb with the
merchant's chest going over the edge, pulls the party through a snowbound
investigation as the pilgrims disappear, and ends in the dragon's lair. See
[[Arc_Shape]].

## Teaser

You were hired in Harrowford to see Master Orrin Gilt, cloth merchant, up
the pass to Saint Aubren's Rest before the snow comes. He pays well. He
talks a great deal. And he really does care about that chest.
