## Description

A single sheet of cheap valley paper, folded small and soft from a pocket. Written in pencil, in a careful hand that presses too hard.

## The Text

> Tam.
>
> If the brothers want paying, don't pay it. I'd sooner have you home than have the cure.
>
> Home by spring, either way.
>
> Your Hesta

## Related

- **People:** [[Tam Rowe]] — carried it up the pass in his coat

## GM Notes

### What It Reveals

Hesta knew the house might ask a price, and told Tam not to pay it. It lands on the shard question and the dragon's bargain: she asked for him, not the cure.

### Delivery

Found in [[Tam Rowe]]'s coat, among the pilgrims' packs in the dragon's hoard ([[What Sleeps Below]]; Scene 2 of [[Session 04 - Long Night Early - Plan]]).

### Hidden Ties

- [[Grenna Fallowmere]] — Hesta's healer, carrying three shards meant for her
