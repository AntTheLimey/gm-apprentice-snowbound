# Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias - Play Notes

Start: Brannoc spent 1 Hit Die (5+2), 41/44. Nim still Exhaustion 1.
Kael back through the gate, Hollis shut it behind him. Soup.

Party split after breakfast. Ilyra to the tower with Durgan, Brannoc to Crane, Kael to Hollis about Tam, Nim "for a walk".

Kael at Hollis's desk, asked to see when Tam left. Read the register in front of Hollis. Found "Tam Rowe" with a little lamp drawn by it, fresh ink. Investigation 7, didn't see the pattern. Asked about the lamp: "Guests we pray for, child. Every one." Kael didn't ask anything else.

Tower: Durgan showed Ilyra the chiselled lamps. Rubbing matches. Durgan: "Someone with a chisel and no love for it. Not a mason." Only whole lamp left is over the abbot's door. Durgan pointed out the blocked stair, said leave it, it's loud work. Ilyra left it. Top of the tower, Perception 17: steam rising from a vent in the glacier, right below the chapter house.

Brannoc with Crane by the fire. Crane's report: something heavy dragged past his door night 1, going down. Footsteps from the merchant's room every hour all night. "A fat man who walks like a thin one." Brannoc asked him to listen tonight.

Nim went after Gilt's purse in the cloister. Bumped him, Sleight of Hand 15 vs his passive 15, got it. Felt a thin man under the coat. Purse: 14 gp and a little pewter badge, the whole lamp.
NEW-ITEM: Gilt's pewter lamp badge — in his purse, the whole lamp. Nim kept the purse till the unmasking.

Lunch in the refectory, everyone back. Kael told them about Tam's name and the lamp mark. Nim told them Gilt is "thin as a rake under all that". Ilyra: steam, chisels. Argument. Brannoc wanted to go straight to the abbot. Ilyra said not without proof. Nim said they should go when the snow lets them. Kael: the crypt door.
Severin came by the table and asked Gilt to fetch the lamp oil, "you'll know where." Gilt went and came back with it. Brannoc Insight 13, nothing odd.

Afternoon. Kael + Brannoc to the inner stair. Teodor on the bottom step carving a candle, stood up. Brannoc tried to talk past him, Persuasion 2. Teodor didn't move or answer. Kael: "We'll come back." They decided to come back during the office.
Ilyra to the chapel. Ansel rehearsing the office with Pip holding the music. Ilyra Religion 19: it's the old evening hymn of the Lantern at the Pass, with "breath" sung where "light" should be. Asked Ansel why. "Father Severin chose the old hymn for tonight." Ansel: "The abbot never looked at me the way he looks at that merchant."
Nim slept the afternoon in the guest wing. Not a long rest.

Dusk, the office for Brother Matthias. Brannoc and Ilyra went, with Gilt. Kael and Nim didn't.
Severin hummed the hymn behind Gilt. Ilyra Insight 8, saw nothing in Gilt.
Marda sang "light" out loud where the brothers sang "breath". Ilyra noticed.
Severin announced the Long Night vigil, the night after tomorrow, "to honour our guest the merchant". Gilt said thank you.
Teodor stood at the back by the door the whole office.

Kael and Nim at the crypt door during the office. Nim on the lock, thieves' tools nat 1. Pick snapped off in the lock. Tried with the spare, still nothing by the time the singing stopped. Left in a hurry. Never got in. The broken tip is still in the lock.
Kael and Nim missed at the office.

Supper. Gilt loud about his purse: "Somebody in this house is a thief!" Nim ate her soup.

Night 2. Hollis brought cups round in the guest wing. Kael Perception 18: Gilt's cup poured from a small separate jug. Kael knocked it out of Gilt's hand, "clumsy". Hollis: "Never mind, child," and didn't bring another.
Ilyra drank hers. Nim pocketed hers. Brannoc said he'd keep watch, didn't drink.
Brannoc on watch, Perception 16 vs Teodor's Stealth 24, didn't see him go by.
After midnight: Gilt shouted from his room, "Not tonight, Teodor." Everyone up. Teodor in Gilt's doorway with a rope and Hollis's lamp. Teodor stepped back, said nothing.
Gilt tore off his hat. Thin man, older, in the oiled mail and the white surcoat with the whole lamp, the one from the chest. Pilgrims in the doorways: Marda, Crane, Durgan, Rosie.
Severin came down the corridor with his lamp, Hollis and Ansel behind him.
Gilt: "I am Lucan. You taught me the hymn, Father."
Gilt accused him in front of everyone: the cups, carrying guests down to rest, feeding them to the dragon under the glacier for the cure; the register marks are the abbot's list, "and you wrote me first"; they gave up the Lantern and cut it off the walls.
Gilt pointed to the register mark by Tam's name (Kael), the steam (Ilyra), the chiselled lamps.
Kael: "Where's Tam?" Gilt: "Below. With the others."
Nim gave Gilt his purse back. "I think you dropped this."
Severin didn't deny any of it. "Peace, child. Someone must keep the house alive." The brothers closed round him. Pilgrims looked at their cups.
Roll initiative. End.
