## Session Intent

An open investigation through day 2 at [[Saint Aubren's Rest]]: every lead is on the table and the players choose where to dig. The monks react to what they touch. However the day goes, it ends with Gilt's unveiling, his accusation of the abbot, and "Roll initiative".

## Session Overview

Snowed in with a house of kind monks, a missing brother-in-law and a funeral for a man who never lived. The party can read the register, climb the tower, listen to the blind sergeant, push the infirmarian or try the frosted door. Every one of those is noticed by someone. Dusk brings Brother Matthias's office, and the old hymn behind Gilt's chair. What the night brings depends on what the party did with the day.

## Previously On...

The mule nearly went over the drop with Gilt's chest, and Gilt cared only for the chest. On the Widow's Stair Gilt knew the turn before Kael did. At the wayside shrine four ice mephits came out of the snow; Gilt healed Brannoc with a murmured word and called it luck. The shrine's carving showed the whole lamp, and Ilyra kept a silver lamp worked with it. At Saint Aubren's Rest each of them found someone they knew: Tam, Crane, Durgan, Rosie. At supper the abbot asked to keep Ilyra's lamp, and she said no. In the night Brannoc met Brother Teodor carrying a sack down to a frosted door, and Nim found mail, a sword and a lamp surcoat in Gilt's chest. In the morning Tam was gone with no tracks. The abbot said a Brother Matthias had died, and invited them all to his office tonight.

## Active Threads

- **Tam Rowe is gone.** No tracks; his pack and Hesta's salve were under his bed. Hollis says he left before dawn.
  Kael carries the salve. Tam's boot is in [[The Ice Crypt]].
- **What Teodor carried down the inner stair.** Severin says Brother Matthias. There is no Matthias; the office tonight is over an empty shroud.
  Brannoc saw Teodor at the frosted door.
- **What is in Gilt's chest, and why.** Mail, a longsword, a white surcoat with the whole lamp. Nim and Ilyra know; the others don't.
  Gilt doesn't know they know.
- **The whole lamp.** The shrine and the silver votive lamp show it uncut; the house has chiselled it off. Severin wants Ilyra's lamp for the chapel.
  Durgan has promised Ilyra the tower, where the last uncut carving hangs over the abbot's door.
- **Gilt's "luck".** He heals with a prayer and knows the Widow's Stair.
  Crane hears footsteps from his room every hour, all night.

## Contingency Scenes

### The Party Digs Where the Monks Notice

**Trigger:** Any "noticed" row above: the register read openly, the crypt door tried, Wren pushed, the tower stair opened.
**Then**
- Hollis or Ansel carries it to Severin within the hour.
- Severin moves Gilt's turn up to tonight ([[The Second Night]]).
- The house stays exactly as warm; the cups come round as usual.

### The Party Confronts Gilt

**Trigger:** The PCs put enough to Gilt: the chest, the healing, the hymn, the footsteps, the register.
**Then**
- He tells them everything: Lucan, the runaway novice; the Lantern at the Pass; the dragon; the bargain.
- He asks for their help; whatever they answer, the unmasking follows (Contingency: Gilt Unmasks).

### Gilt Goes Digging

**Trigger:** The party sits still through day 2.
**Then**
- During the office or after it, Gilt slips away to the crypt stair or the register and is nearly caught.
- The PCs can cover for him or not; either way it is noticed.

### Gilt Unmasks

**Trigger:** Whichever comes first: the PCs confront Gilt and he joins them; Teodor comes for him; or about 15 minutes of play are left. In that case Gilt pulls the PCs together himself ("All of you. Now. Bring your swords.") and leads them to the abbot.
**Then**
- He tears off the hat and stands thin, in oiled mail, with the white lamp surcoat, before the abbot and the house: "I am Lucan. You taught me the hymn, Father."
- He accuses Severin, to the pilgrims as much as to the monks:
  - **Murder of guests:** the cups at supper, carried "down to rest" in the night. Tam Rowe last night; Marda tonight, if she went.
  - **The bargain:** the bodies go down the chute to the dragon under the glacier, and its breath in the ice cures the order's sickness. Wren's shards are its breath.
  - **The roster:** the lamp marks in the register are the abbot's list of who dies next. "And you wrote me first."
  - **The god they gave up:** the Lantern at the Pass, chiselled off every wall but the one over the abbot's door.
- He names whatever the party has actually found as his proof (the register, the boot or the body, the shards, the steam vent); what they haven't found, he asserts.
- Severin doesn't deny it: "Peace, child. Someone must keep the house alive." The brothers close round their abbot; the pilgrims look at their cups.
- **The session ends on the GM's line: "Roll initiative."** The fight itself is session 3.
