## Narrative Recap

"Then I will have Long Night early," said [[The Dragon Under the Glacier|the Guest]], and uncoiled off its coin.

The roof came down first. Icicles fell on [[Silver Votive Lamp|the lamp]], and on [[Master Orrin Gilt|Lucan]] and [[Grenna Fallowmere|Grenna]] and [[Ilyra Vessant|Ilyra]], and on [[Brannoc Hale|Brannoc]] out on the coin. [[Nim Underbough|Nim]] put an arrow past its head. [[Kael Stonehand|Kael]] put Hunter's Mark on it, the thing that had eaten [[Tam Rowe|Tam]], and the first arrow went in. Lucan sang, and blessed Brannoc and [[Oswin]] and Nim. Brannoc walked out across forty winters of frozen coin to its foreleg and hit it three times. It raked him with one claw, and [[The Abbot's Ring|the abbot's ring]] took the cold out of the wound. Oswin ran across the coin barefoot and couldn't land a blow. It beat its wings, and both of them kept their feet, and it went up in a storm of loose coin and came down ten feet from the lamp.

![[chapter-01-session-04-on-the-coin.png]]

It breathed on the lamp. Ilyra and Grenna turned their faces into the light and lived through it. Lucan didn't. He went down in the white, and lay on the coin at its feet. Grenna said a word and he got up, and the green of her magic closed Ilyra's frostbite. Ilyra stepped up the slope through nothing, beside Kael, and put fire on it, and it shrugged half of it off.

![[chapter-01-session-04-breath-on-the-lamp.png]]

Then the arrows started landing. Nim, Kael, Kael. The coin floor tilted under Lucan and Grenna and slid them down to its head. Lucan got up and the lamp-light spirits wheeled round him. Brannoc drank his potion and came back across the coin to stand over the priest. Oswin hit it so hard it staggered.

It stopped. It lifted its head, bleeding, and spoke quietly over the hymn. Spare it, it said, and it would breathe for their sick, the woman in the valley and the brothers, every winter, and take only the dead who died on their own. One of them could keep its house. "Kill me, and the ones already blue have [[The Ice Shards|six shards]] between them."

"Six shards is two cures," said Grenna. "There are four of them blue." Kael turned his head on the slope. "Four?" "[[Hesta Rowe|Hesta]]'s one. I have three in my coat. I took them for her." Kael said Tam had come up here to buy her life off them, and look where it had put him, and nocked another arrow. Nim offered it half the coin. It said no. "Then no," said Nim. Oswin said the office's words: "Peace, Guest. Rest." Brannoc said terms were for armies. "You ate a boy in his shirt." Lucan never stopped singing.

"Then I will keep what I can carry." It breathed down the coin again, over Lucan and Brannoc and Grenna and Oswin. Lucan fell. Brannoc's ring held. Oswin and Grenna both found something left in them; Oswin kept his feet, and Grenna went down to one knee on nothing and refused to fall. It took Lucan in its jaws, gently, like a dog with a pup, and went down the coin into the meltwater and under the ice.

Grenna, on her knees, said a word to herself, and a word under the ice, and Lucan opened his eyes in the dragon's mouth. Ilyra put three rays of fire down through the ice. It held on.

Kael came down the slope at a run with Tam's mark still on it and shot twice through the ice at the white shape. It stopped ten feet short of the glacier wall and let Lucan go. The cold went out of [[The Dragon's Cavern|the cavern]] in one long breath, like a door left open. Lucan came up through the broken ice, choking, and Brannoc waded in to his waist and hauled him out by the surcoat. Kael lowered his bow and said nothing.

![[chapter-01-session-04-through-the-ice.png]]

They rested on the coin. Lucan sat down and wept, once, into his hands. Nim went through the pilgrims' packs before anybody else thought to. She found a coat with a valley stitch at the cuff and didn't make a joke; she gave it to Kael. In the pocket was [[Hesta's Letter|a letter from Hesta]]. Kael read it out. "If the brothers want paying, don't pay it. I'd sooner have you home than have the cure. Home by spring, either way." He looked at Grenna. "She'd say don't pay for it. Give her them anyway." "I was going to," said Grenna.

Oswin walked along the wall of the dead and said the office over every one of them, with "light". One was the old cellarer who had died in Oswin's first winter, the one who taught him the stairs. Ilyra read the tags at their collars. Every one had a little lamp scratched on it.

Brannoc worked [[Frost Brand|a frost-white longsword]] out of the ice. Kael took [[Boots of the Winterlands|the boots]] off a frozen pilgrim after Oswin had prayed over him, and Ilyra took [[Cloak of Protection|a grey cloak]] from a pack. They spent an hour prising coin out of the floor. Nim counted it twice. "Tam's share goes to the valley," she said, and nobody argued. Kael carried Tam up the chute on the rope. He slipped halfway, and Oswin took the other end, and they brought him up together.

Dawn came grey into [[The Cloister|the cloister]]. [[Aldous Crane|Crane]] was at the head of the guest stair with his sword across his knees. "Hale. Report." "Dragon dead. Priest alive. One recovered, deceased." "Good report." [[Pip Callow|Pip]] ran past Brannoc to Oswin.

[[Sister Wren|Wren]] asked Grenna for the shards back. Grenna said three were promised, and Wren's were hers to choose. Wren chose the younger of her two sick brothers. Then she cried, and then she went on working.

Oswin unbarred [[The Chapter House|the chapter house]]. [[Brother Ansel]] asked one thing: "Does the Guest live?" "No." Ansel sat down on the floor. The brothers looked at Oswin. "I'll keep the lamp lit till spring," he said. "Then you choose an abbot who carries nobody."

![[chapter-01-session-04-the-lamp-kept-lit.png]]

[[Brother Hollis]] wanted to read every name in the register aloud. Ilyra sat with him and wrote them down for the families below. He read until noon. In [[The Chapel|the chapel]] Nim found [[Rosie Tallowby|Rosie]] with a knife and a bag of gold leaf. They settled on a third. Kael laid Tam out in the chapel with his coat over him. Lucan and [[Marda Vell|Marda]] and [[Durgan Flintmere|Durgan]] began to cut the whole lamp back into the stone over the abbot's door.

At the first break in the weather Kael took Grenna down [[The Pass|the pass]], a month early, in the dead pilgrim's boots. Four days. Three shards over three nights, and the blue left Hesta's lips. In spring the pass opened. Hollis and the old brother died in it, as Wren had known they would. Nobody new was sick. The brothers chose Wren. Everyone else walked down.
