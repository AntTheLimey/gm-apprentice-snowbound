## Session Intent

The Guest, the lamp, Lucan's life, and what six shards are worth once the dragon offers to keep the sick alive. Everything is on the table: fight, bargain, flight or a mix of them.

## Session Overview

Before dawn on night 2, in the dragon's cavern under the glacier. The party has refused the Guest's price, and it has said "Then I will have Long Night early." The session opens on initiative. Up the chute, the house waits for dawn: the order is barred in the chapter house, the sick brothers are in the infirmary with Wren, and the pass is shut until spring.

## GM Notes

- **Lucan doesn't die to the dice.** He is the arc's key NPC: at 0 HP he stays down and stable, unless the party chooses to leave him. If that happens in play, mark it as a GM fudge in the Play Notes.
- **What the dragon wants, in order:** Lucan eaten, then the lamp out, then to live. It bargains once, when Bloodied, and it keeps a bargain to the letter.
- **If it dies:** its cold stops rising and nobody new sickens. The four already sick ([[Brother Hollis]], two brothers in the infirmary, Hesta in the valley) have six shards between them, enough to cure two. See [[health]].
- **If it gets away:** it is under the glacier with whatever hit points it has left. It comes up through the chapter house floor at the next Long Rest, as its note's Long Rest ruling says, and the whole house counts as its lair.
- **Advancement:** level 6 at the end of the session (milestone, after the abbot and the dragon). Nobody levels up before the fight.

## Contingency Scenes

### Lucan Falls

**Trigger:** Lucan drops to 0 HP.
**Then**
- The dragon spends its next move taking him in its jaws and heads for the meltwater.
- It drops him if it takes 20 or more damage in one turn, or if someone wins a Strength (Athletics) contest against it (it has +4) to pull him free.
- He stays down and stable. He doesn't die to the dice.

### The Lamp Goes Out

**Trigger:** whoever holds the [[Silver Votive Lamp]] fails a save against Cold Breath, or drops to 0 HP.
**Then**
- The lamp gutters out, and nobody has Advantage on saves against the cold any more. The dragon says: "There. Now it is a house again."
- Lucan, or anyone who knows the prayer, can relight it as an action.

### The Party Retreats Up the Chute

**Trigger:** the party runs for the slope and the chute.
**Then**
- The slope is slippery: Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 12, or fall Prone and slide back 10 ft.
- The dragon doesn't follow up the chute. It burrows, and comes up through the chapter house floor within the hour, as its note's Long Rest ruling says. The brothers are barred in there.

### Everyone Falls

**Trigger:** every PC is down.
**Then**
- The dragon takes Lucan and the lamp, and leaves the rest to the cold.
- The downed PCs wake stable at dawn, each with 1 more level of Exhaustion. Wren and Crane are at the foot of the chute with rope.
