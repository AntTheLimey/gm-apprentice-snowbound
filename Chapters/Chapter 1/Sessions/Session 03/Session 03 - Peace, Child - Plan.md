## Session Intent

The fight in the guest wing corridor, a short rest while the party roams the opened house, then Lucan's call to go down tonight: the descent into the glacier and the dragon. The party, now six with [[Oswin]] and [[Grenna Fallowmere]], decides how the fight ends, what they do in the house, which way they go down and what they say to the dragon. The session ends on "Roll initiative" under the glacier; session 4 is the dragon fight.

## Session Overview

Lucan stands unmasked and the abbot has not denied a word. A novice of the house and the valley healer arrive at either end of the corridor, and in round three the rest of the order comes up the inner stair. After the fight the house lies open (the reliquary, the crypt, the register, the pilgrims) until Lucan lights a lamp under the one uncut carving and calls them all to go down before Long Night. Down the crypt chute past the dead, or down the old Glacier Stair through ice and breath, to a cavern of coin and frozen pilgrims where the Guest of the house is waiting to name its price.

## GM Notes

- **Fight budget (six PCs at level 5, plus Lucan):** Severin 2,300, Teodor 700, Wren 700, Hollis 50, Ansel 50, four Brothers of the Open Hand 400 = 4,200 XP. Moderate for six (4,500), more with Severin's lair and villain actions; just under deadly by the Lazy DM benchmark (CR 14.5 against 15).
- **Severin never harms [[Oswin]]**, and Teodor pulls his blows on him ([[Oswin]]'s GM Notes).
- **The crypt key** is on Teodor's belt; the abbot's ring opens every door in the house.
- **Wren's reliquary** holds six ice shards. Each heals as her Ice Shard does; three, melted into a sick person over three nights, cure the order's sickness. Out of the cold they melt within a week.
- **Heroic Inspiration after the corridor fight:** give it to two PCs, for the two best moments of the fight (the boldest, and the one the table cheered). Heroic Inspiration keeps until used, so it carries into the dragon fight in session 4. A PC who already has it can give the new one to another PC (SRD).
- **The abbot's loot** is in [[The Second Night]]'s Treasure: a Ring of Warmth, two Potions of Resistance (Cold), two Potions of Greater Healing, and in his cell a Spell Scroll of *Protection from Energy* and 180 gp. Weigh it in session 4's dragon tuning.
- **Long Night page in the register:** the vigil night is already written up in Hollis's hand: "Master Orrin Gilt, to rest. Oswin, to carry."

## Previously On...

The party spent a snowed-in day digging. Kael found Tam's name in the guest register with a little lamp beside it. Ilyra saw every lamp carving chiselled off the walls but one, and steam rising from the glacier. Crane told Brannoc the merchant walks like a thin man; Nim lifted Gilt's purse and felt it. At the office for a brother who never lived, the house sang "breath" for "light", and the abbot announced a vigil for Long Night in the merchant's honour. That night Kael knocked away Gilt's drugged cup. After midnight Teodor came for Gilt with a rope, and Gilt tore off his hat: "I am Lucan. You taught me the hymn, Father." He told the guest wing what the house does. The abbot did not deny it.

## Contingency Scenes

### The Abbot Goes Below

### Oswin Sides With the House

**Trigger:** Oswin fights for the abbot.
**Then**
- Nobody in the order harms him; Lucan pleads with him by name.
- If the abbot falls, Oswin is the one the brothers look to.

### The Party Tries to Long Rest

**Trigger:** The party settles down for a Long Rest anywhere in the house instead of going down (GM ruling, Ant, 2026-11-18).
**Then**
- About an hour in, they get a Short Rest's benefit and no more.
- The floor of [[The Chapter House]] heaves and splits: [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]] claws its way up from the glacier through the stone, breathing steam. The brothers barred in there are first in its way; their screaming wakes the house.
- **It opens with a free Cold Breath** before initiative is rolled, as it comes up through the floor, at whoever is nearest (the barred brothers, and anyone resting or keeping watch close by). This stands in for the cold and hazards the party skipped by not going down.
- **The whole house is its lair:** it was the first guest, and its cold has been in the stones for forty winters. Its lair actions work here, recast: Icefall comes off the cloister eaves; Freezing Fog is steam from the broken floor; The Coin Slides is the flagstones heaving. Legendary Resistance and Legendary Actions as tuned.
- Session 4's fight happens in the chapter house and the cloister instead of the cavern; the treasure stays below.

### Lucan Falls

**Trigger:** Lucan drops to 0 HP.
**Then**
- Severin stops for a breath: "Rest, child." He kneels by Lucan if nobody stops him.
- Lucan makes death saves; if he dies, the brothers stop fighting for a round.
