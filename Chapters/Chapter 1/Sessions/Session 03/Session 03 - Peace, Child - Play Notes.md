### Balance, for the GM

- Lucan was the weak point: 38 HP and AC 16 in the line of a 6d6 cone, with every enemy told to go for him first. He goes down to the dragon on one short rest and a potion.

### What Sleeps Below

> Under the glacier the ice is blue all the way through, and lit from nowhere. Coins stand in it like seeds. There are people in the walls, upright, in their travelling clothes. Something white lies on the coin with its eyes shut, and the cold comes off it in slow breaths you can count.

- The skitter of ice reaches the coins. One eye opens. It does not lift its head.
- **The Guest:** "I was the first guest of this house. Before the saint, before his lamp. They built their charity on my roof." It looks at the lamp in Ilyra's hand for a long time. "And now you bring that down my stair."
- **To Lucan:** "You ran. Your teacher wrote you first. I would have kept you with the others." Then, to Oswin: "And you were to carry him."
- **The sickness:** "Their sickness is my breath, through the ice. I give it, and for the dead I take it back. That is a bargain, little ones. That is what a house is." Grenna's hand goes to her coat.

#### The offers, one each

- **Grenna:** "The woman in the valley. Hesta. Three nights of my breath and the blue leaves her lips." Grenna, Morgan: "I've already got three nights in my coat." The Guest, amused: "Three shards for one woman. And next winter, healer? Who will carry me the one after?" Kael turns and looks at Grenna. She doesn't look back.
- **Kael:** "Your sister's husband. His bones, and his coat, carried up whole for the burying." Kael: "I'll carry him myself."
- **Oswin:** "The house. An abbot who carries." Oswin, Dev, very quietly, in the office's words: "Peace, Guest. Rest." The Guest does not like it.
- **Nim:** "A share of forty winters, little thief. And every name you owe, laid in the ice." Jo: Nim haggles: "Half. And the names first." It enjoys this. Nim watches it while it talks (Insight 10-1 = 9 vs DC 15, failure). She can't read anything in it at all.
- **Brannoc:** "Terms, soldier. Leave me the priest, and every guest walks down in spring." Brannoc: "My company doesn't leave anyone." It laughs at the word company. Lucan says nothing.
- **Ilyra:** "The saint's true story, every word they cut off the walls. And put out that lamp." Ilyra, Alex, precise: "First tell me what the saint called you." The Guest: "Guest." Ilyra: "Then I know enough."

#### Its price

- "Lucan. That lamp, put out. And one of you to keep my house."
- Nobody takes a bargain. Ilyra raises the whole lamp over her head. The Guest's eyes close against it, and it stops speaking. Lucan starts the evening hymn, with "light", and Oswin sings it with him.
- **The Guest:** "Then I will have Long Night early."
