# Peace, Child

Date: Nov 18th, 2026

## Summary
"Peace, child," said the abbot. "Rest." Then the corridor was a fight.

Nim threw first. Her dagger took Brother Hollis in the throat of his habit, and he folded over the guest register he was carrying. Two new faces were in the corridor that night: Oswin, a goliath novice of the house, who had come up behind the brothers with a lamp, and Grenna Fallowmeer, the healer from Kael's valley, who had been nursing in the infirmary all week and came up the guest stair at the shouting. Oswin did not fight. He walked through the brothers, who let him, and stood between the abbot and Lucan. "Father. Is it true?" The abbot told him to come here. He did not move.

Brannoc blew his old company whistle and went at Brother Teodor in the doorway. Four sword strokes, and three of them landed. Down the corridor a door banged, and blind Aldous Crane came out with his greatsword, swinging at the sound of habits. Ilyra tried to hold the abbot with a spell and he smiled at her. The brothers took up the evening hymn, with "breath", and the corridor got colder.

Grenna put a column of moonlight on the abbot. The abbot sent two bolts of cold at Lucan over Oswin's shoulder; one found him, and Lucan went to his knees. Kael put Hunter's Mark on Teodor, the man who had carried Tam down, and shot him twice. Teodor fell, and Crane, blind, hacked at the sound of him falling. Lucan called up spirits of lamp-light and they burned the brothers. Then the abbot spoke names from the register, gently, and Lucan, Brannoc and Nim were afraid of him.

In the second round Oswin made up his mind. "No, Father." He hit the abbot, and kept hitting him, and the abbot never once raised a hand to him. Nim put a second dagger into Brother Ansel. The floor breathed cold, and Lucan fell. Grenna got him up with a word, and the abbot breathed the dragon's cold straight down the corridor at him, and he fell again. The abbot spoke a word over Brother Ansel, who got up off the floor and knelt beside Lucan with his mace. The blow glanced off the oiled mail. Lucan lay in Gilt's doorway in the white surcoat with the whole lamp on it, barely breathing.

The rest of the order came up the inner stair: Sister Wren, four young brothers, and Pip with a lamp. Ilyra threw a web across the head of the stair and caught three of them. The abbot breathed again, and Brannoc went down. Grenna pulled him back, and Nim too. Oswin knocked the abbot flat on his own frost and beat him down to almost nothing. He died in Grenna's moonlight on the flags of his own guest wing. "Someone must keep the house alive," he said, and looked past Oswin at Pip, and stopped. Wren sat down on the stair and cried. The brothers stopped.

After the fight, the house was quiet. Grenna got to Lucan first and kept him breathing. Marda Vell knelt by him and sang the hymn with "light". Grenna saved Brother Hollis's life too. Brannoc had the brothers walked to the chapter house and barred in, and gave Crane the guest wing. In the infirmary Wren opened her reliquary for Grenna: six shards of ice. "Three nights, three shards, and the blue leaves the lips." Grenna took three and didn't say why.

Kael took the crypt key off Teodor. Nim got her broken pick out of the lock first. Inside were empty niches, drag marks to a chute, and Tam's boot. Hesta had stitched the cuff. Oswin and Ilyra read the register. On the Long Night page, in Hollis's hand: "Master Orrin Gilt, to rest. Oswin, to carry." "I copied these," Oswin said. "I drew the lamps." He told Pip the truth and left him with Marda.

They rested by the fire. Grenna poured one of the abbot's healing potions into Lucan, and he opened his eyes and asked if the abbot was dead. Nim gave him back his hat: "I think you dropped this." Brannoc put on the abbot's ring. Nobody used a shard.

Lucan told them why he ran: at nineteen he was told the bargain and asked to carry. He looked at Oswin a long time. "He was raising you to carry. As he raised me."

Lucan lit a lamp under the one carving the house had left whole, over the abbot's door, and sang the hymn with "light". "We go down tonight. I can't do it alone." He asked Ilyra for the silver lamp from the wayside shrine. She kept it and lit it herself, and he said the prayer, and it burned. Nim wanted to wait for spring. Grenna said the sick die in spring. Kael said Tam was down there. They went down the chute.

Tam was at the bottom, in the ice, in his shirt. Kael put the boot beside him. They went round a pool of thin ice on the rope, and Ilyra slipped on the slope below it, and the ice skittered down onto a floor of coin.

Something white was asleep on the coin. One eye opened. "I was the first guest of this house," it said. It told Lucan his teacher had written him first. It told them the sickness was its breath. It offered Grenna three nights for Hesta, and Kael Tam's bones, and Oswin the house, and Nim forty winters of coin, and Brannoc safe passage for every guest, and Ilyra the saint's story if she put out the lamp. Its price was Lucan, the lamp, and a new abbot. Nobody took it. Ilyra held the lamp up. "Then I will have Long Night early," said the Guest.

## Scenes
### The Fight in the Corridor
#### The abbot and his brothers against the party, Lucan and two newcomers.
Nim drops Hollis with a thrown dagger. Oswin refuses to fight and stands between the abbot and Lucan. Brannoc blows his whistle; Crane joins blind. Kael and Brannoc drop Teodor and Crane kills him. The abbot's cold drops Lucan in round two; Ansel's mace glances off him. Oswin turns on the abbot. Ilyra webs the reinforcements on the stair. The abbot dies in Grenna's Moonbeam in round three, and the order yields.

### After the Fight
#### The party splits up through the house.
Grenna stabilises Lucan and Hollis. Brannoc bars the brothers in the chapter house and leaves Crane on the guest wing. Grenna takes three shards from Wren's reliquary. Kael and Nim open the crypt and find Tam's boot. Oswin and Ilyra read the register's Long Night page. Oswin tells Pip the truth.

### The Short Rest
#### Wounds bound, loot shared out by the guest wing fire.
A potion wakes Lucan. Brannoc takes the abbot's ring. The party shares out the potions, a scroll and the alms box.

### The Call
#### A lamp under the carving, and the silver lamp lit.
Lucan lights a lamp under the carving and asks for the silver lamp; Ilyra lights it herself. The party argues about going down, and goes.

### The Chute
#### Down to the ice cave and Tam.
A belayed slide down the crypt chute; the cold; Tam's body; thin ice; a slip on the slope.

### The Guest
#### The dragon talks before it fights.
It names itself the first guest, says the sickness is its breath, makes an offer to each of them, and names its price. They refuse. Initiative.
