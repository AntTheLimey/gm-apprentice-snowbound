## Session Intent

Getting there is three quarters of the story: the climb up [[The Pass]] with [[Master Orrin Gilt]] is a hard, eventful day. The last quarter is settling in at [[Saint Aubren's Rest]], and the session ends at breakfast with a pilgrim missing.

## Session Overview

A day's climb from [[Harrowford]] to [[Saint Aubren's Rest]]: a mule, a chest and an ice shelf; spiteful Ice Mephits at the wayside shrine; the monastery's lamps at dusk. A warm welcome, a supper, a night of snow. At breakfast, [[Tam Rowe]]'s place is empty.

## GM Notes

- Truth of the campaign: see [[Snowbound]] (the brief), [[Severins_Clock]] and [[Arc_Shape]].
- Gilt is Lucan in a [[Hat of Disguise]]. This session his tells are small: he knows the path, his "luck" heals a wound, he goes still at the hymn.

## Planned Scenes

### Scene 1: The Ice Shelf

**Type:** chase
**Situation:** Mid-morning on [[The Pass]]; the mule slips on the first ice shelf and goes over, and [[Master Orrin Gilt]]'s locked chest swings out over the drop on its rope.
**Starts it:** Gilt shrieks "Mind the chest! The mule can look after itself!" and grabs the nearest PC's arm.
**Entities:** [[Master Orrin Gilt]], [[The Pass]], [[Map of the Pass]], [[Map of the Pass (GM)]]
**NPCs**
- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]]:** wants the chest saved, at any cost. Does nothing useful if left alone; flaps and shouts.
**Points to land**
- [ ] Gilt cares more for the chest than the mule.
- [ ] The chest is far heavier than bolts of cloth should be.
**If the players...**
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Haul the rope | Strength (Athletics) DC 15; two helpers give Advantage. Fail: the rope slips a yard, try again with a level of risk |
| Save the mule first | Gilt is furious, then the chest's rope frays: one round left |
| Climb down to it | Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 13 on the ice; fail: slide, 1d6 bludgeoning, catch the ledge |
| Ask what's in it | "Samples. Fine wool. Very fine." It doesn't sound like wool |
| Nobody acts in two rounds | The chest drops onto a ledge 20 ft down: recoverable, with a climb |

> The path narrows to a shelf of grey ice above a long fall. Hooves scrabble, a rope snaps taut, and something heavy swings out over the drop, turning slowly on its line.

### Scene 2: The Climb

**Type:** transition
**Situation:** The long middle of the day; the weather is turning and [[Kael Stonehand]] has to choose the route.
**Starts it:** Kael sees cloud building over the col by noon: the long switchbacks, or the steep Widow's Stair?
**Entities:** [[The Pass]], [[Master Orrin Gilt]], [[Map of the Pass]], [[Map of the Pass (GM)]]
**Points to land**
- [ ] The weather is closing; they must reach the monastery today.
- [ ] Gilt knows the path better than a merchant should (he names a turn before Kael does).
**If the players...**
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Take the switchbacks | Safe, slow: they reach the shrine late and the monastery after dark |
| Take the Widow's Stair | Group Strength (Athletics) or Wisdom (Survival) DC 13. Pass: shrine by midday. Fail: one level of Exhaustion each for those who failed, until a Long Rest |
| Ask Gilt how he knows the way | "I've studied maps. I'm very thorough." |

### Scene 3: The Wayside Shrine

**Type:** combat
**Situation:** A wind-scoured roadside shrine carved with an old lamp; four [[Ice Mephit]]s nest in the drifts behind it.
**Starts it:** The mule balks; a drift sits up and laughs.
**Entities:** [[Ice Mephit]], [[The Pass]], [[The Lantern at the Pass]], [[Battle Map of the Wayside Shrine]], [[Map of the Pass]], [[Map of the Pass (GM)]]
**NPCs**
- **[[Ice Mephit]] (4):** want warm things to bite and mock. Flee at one left.
- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]]:** hangs back; after the fight he "prays" over the worst-wounded PC and the wound closes. "Luck," he says.
**Points to land**
- [ ] The shrine's carving is the whole lamp: the same design as [[Ilyra Vessant]]'s rubbing, uncut.
- [ ] Gilt's prayer heals.
**Treasure**
- Potion of Healing, in the shrine's offering niche under the snow
- Potion of Healing, in the same niche
- Potion of Resistance (Cold), in the same niche
- 38 gp in old coin, in the same niche
- A small silver votive lamp, 25 gp, showing the whole lamp the order has chiselled off its own walls.
**If the players...**
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Use fire | Mephits are Vulnerable to Fire |
| Stand next to a dying mephit | Death Burst: Con save DC 10, 2d4 Cold (half on success) |
| Compare the carving to the rubbing | It matches; here nothing is chiselled off |
| Question Gilt's "luck" | He blusters, changes the subject to the weather |
| Search the shrine | Investigation DC 10 finds the offering niche and the treasure |

> Snow has drifted against a roadside shrine, a squat stone hood over a carved lamp, its flame worn smooth by wind. One of the drifts sits up.

### Scene 4: The Last Mile

**Type:** transition
**Situation:** Dusk, cloud and the first flakes; the monastery's lamps appear above, a line of lit windows cut into the mountain.
**Starts it:** Kael spots the lamps through the cloud.
**Entities:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]], [[The Pass]], [[Map of Saint Aubren's Rest]], [[Map of Saint Aubren's Rest (GM)]]
**Points to land**
- [ ] Saint Aubren's Rest looks like the safest place in the world.

> The cloud parts for a moment. Above you, cut into the mountain, a ladder of lit windows, and the sound of a bell.

### Scene 5: The Gate and the Guest Wing

**Type:** social
**Situation:** [[Brother Hollis]] opens the gate with a lamp, soup and blankets; the guest wing is warm and full of pilgrims.
**Starts it:** Hollis: "Come in, come in, before it takes your fingers. Sign the book in the morning, or now if you can hold a pen."
**Entities:** [[Brother Hollis]], [[The Guest Wing]], [[Tam Rowe]], [[Aldous Crane]], [[Durgan Flintmere]], [[Rosie Tallowby]], [[Map of the Guest Wing]], [[Map of the Guest Wing (GM)]]
**NPCs**
- **[[Brother Hollis]]:** wants everyone warm, fed and written in the register.
- **[[Tam Rowe]]:** wants news of Hesta; overjoyed to see Kael.
- **[[Aldous Crane]]:** knows Brannoc's voice before he's through the door.
- **[[Durgan Flintmere]]:** delighted Ilyra came; "Wait till you see the tower."
- **[[Rosie Tallowby]]:** delighted to see Nim; asks loudly how the debts are going.
**Points to land**
- [ ] Hollis writes every arrival in the guest register.
- [ ] All four PCs find the person they came for.

### Scene 6: Supper

**Type:** social
**Situation:** The refectory; monks and all six pilgrims at one long table; [[Abbot Severin]] presides.
**Starts it:** Severin rises to say grace.
**Entities:** [[Abbot Severin]], [[Brother Ansel]], [[Brother Hollis]], [[Sister Wren]], [[Brother Teodor]], [[Marda Vell]], [[Tam Rowe]], [[Durgan Flintmere]], [[Pip Callow]], [[Rosie Tallowby]], [[Aldous Crane]], [[Master Orrin Gilt]], [[The Cloister]]
**NPCs**
- **[[Abbot Severin]]:** wants to test Gilt. Hums the old evening hymn behind Gilt's chair; says grace with "light" changed to "breath".
- **[[Brother Ansel]]:** leads the singing; watches the abbot watching Gilt.
- **[[Brother Hollis]]:** serves; brings Tam the sleeping cup.
- **[[Sister Wren]]:** asks after any wounds from the shrine; offers ice-shard salve.
- **[[Brother Teodor]]:** silent at the end of the table; eats little.
- **[[Marda Vell]]:** frowns at the grace and mutters the right word.
- **[[Tam Rowe]]:** sits by Kael; talks about Hesta and nothing else.
- **[[Durgan Flintmere]]:** sketches the hall's carvings on the tablecloth for Ilyra.
- **[[Pip Callow]]:** sits beside Ansel, copying how the monks hold their bowls.
- **[[Rosie Tallowby]]:** works the table with her relic case; makes a point of sitting by Nim.
- **[[Aldous Crane]]:** asks Brannoc to describe the faces round the table.
**Points to land**
- [ ] Gilt goes still at the hymn.
- [ ] Brother Hollis brings [[Tam Rowe]] a cup "to help you sleep".
**If the players...**
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Ask Marda what's wrong | "It's 'by Thy light', not 'breath'. I've said it sixty years." |
| Watch Gilt | Wisdom (Insight) DC 14: he isn't startled; he knows the tune |
| Ask about the order | Severin: a refuge since Saint Aubren; the brothers' old sickness, borne gladly |

### Scene 7: Night, and Breakfast

**Type:** horror
**Situation:** The snow closes the pass in the night. In the morning, Tam's place at breakfast is empty.
**Starts it:** Hollis, at breakfast: "Your friend left before dawn, poor soul. Grief makes men foolish."
**Entities:** [[Brother Hollis]], [[Aldous Crane]], [[Tam Rowe]], [[The Guest Wing]], [[Map of the Guest Wing]], [[Map of the Guest Wing (GM)]]
**Points to land**
- [ ] There are no tracks in the new snow.
- [ ] Tam's things are still under his bed, including the salve Kael gave him.
**If the players...**
| Do | Then |
|---|---|
| Keep watch in the night | Wisdom (Perception) DC 16: something heavy dragged down an inner stair; hourly footsteps from Gilt's room |
| Ask Crane | "Somebody carried something down past my door. Heavy. Twice I thought I heard the merchant walking, every hour." |
| Go after Tam down the pass | The pass is shut; waist-deep drifts, no tracks |

> The snow came in the night. The world outside the windows has gone soft and white and silent, and the long table is laid for breakfast.

## Contingency Scenes

### Somebody Follows Teodor

**Trigger:** A PC on watch hears the dragging and follows it in the night.
**Then**
- [[Brother Teodor]] is on the inner stair with a sack over his shoulder; he says a brother died and is going down to rest.
- He will not let them past the crypt door; he is gentle and immovable.
- The abbot hears of it at breakfast, and the PCs are noticed (see [[Severins_Clock]]).

### Somebody Tries to Leave

**Trigger:** The PCs try to go back down the pass on day 2.
**Then**
- Snow to the waist; a Wisdom (Survival) DC 18 says nobody gets down alive before spring.
