# Session 01 - The Climb - Play Notes

Ice shelf: Kael went for the mule, not the chest. Chest dropped to the ledge; Nim climbed down (Acrobatics 17), heard metal inside. Brannoc + Kael hauled it up. Gilt: "Samples. Fine wool."

Widow's Stair. Group check: Nim failed (Athletics 8), 1 level of Exhaustion until a long rest. Gilt named the left fork above the cairn before Kael did.

Shrine: Ilyra Fireball, sculpted round Brannoc and Kael: mephits 1-3 dead. Brannoc failed both death-burst saves. Mephit 4 fled up the mountain. Gilt healed Brannoc 10 HP; Brannoc said army chaplains do that, called it not luck.
Shrine niche (Nim, Investigation 14): Potion of Healing to Brannoc, Potion of Healing to Kael, Potion of Resistance (Cold) to Nim, 38 gp old coin to the party, silver votive lamp to Ilyra.

Gate: Nim signed the register as "Nell Underhill". Brannoc signed with his old rank.
Nim paid Rosie 10 gp of the old coin to keep quiet about the debts. Party coin now 28 gp.
Kael gave Tam the salve.

Supper: Ilyra put the silver lamp on the table and asked Severin what it was. Severin asked to keep it for the chapel; she said no; he gave it back, smiling. Ruled: Severin now knows the party found the shrine lamp, and who carries it.
Wren offered Brannoc ice salve; he declined.

Night. Brannoc on watch, Perception 18: heard the dragging and followed. Played Somebody Follows Teodor. Teodor: "A brother died. He goes down to rest." Brannoc turned back at the crypt door.
Nim picked Gilt's chest in the storeroom off the guest wing (thieves' tools 21). Ruled what's in it: oiled mail, a longsword, white surcoat with the whole lamp in gold thread on the breast. Gilt came down with a candle, prayed over the lock, went back up; didn't see her (Stealth 19). Nim told Ilyra.
Long rest overnight: Nim's exhaustion gone; everyone back to full HP.
NEW-NPC: Brother Matthias — the abbot named him at breakfast as the brother who died in the night, after a long illness. Office for him this evening, guests invited. Improvised; the abbot's cover for Teodor's sack.
Breakfast: Brannoc asked the whole table which brother died. Ruled: the monks know Brannoc saw Teodor on the stair.
Kael: no tracks anywhere; Tam's pack and Hesta's salve under the bed, Kael took the salve. Kael tried the pass alone, Survival 11, turned back in waist-deep snow.
