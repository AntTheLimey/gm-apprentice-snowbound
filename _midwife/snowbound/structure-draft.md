# Snowbound: structure (confirmed by Ant, 2026-11-16)

Drafted by Prentice, corrected and confirmed by Ant.

## 1. Severin's clock: what happens if nobody acts

Three days (Ant: the climb, the fight and the arrival are one day; a victim
every night). Harrowford is the valley town (confirmed).

| When | What happens | What the PCs can notice |
|------|--------------|-------------------------|
| Day 1, dawn | Master Orrin Gilt has hired the four PCs in Harrowford (the evening before). They set off up the pass. | He paid in old coin, too much of it, up front. Gilt knows the path better than a merchant should. |
| Day 1, midday | Ice Mephits at the wind-scoured shrine with the old lamp carved on it. | Gilt "prays" over a wounded PC; the wound closes. "Luck," he says. |
| Day 1, evening | Arrival at Saint Aubren's Rest. Hollis at the gate. Supper with the pilgrims. Severin's first test: he hums the old evening hymn behind Gilt's chair, and says grace with "light" changed to "breath". | Gilt goes still at the hymn. The widow frowns at the grace. |
| Night 1 | The snow closes the pass. Hollis marks the register. Teodor carries the young husband down to rest (a sleeping draught at supper). | The soldier hears dragging, and hourly footsteps from Gilt's room (the hat). |
| Day 2, morning | The young husband is gone. Hollis: "He left before dawn, poor soul. Grief makes men foolish." **Session 1 ends.** | No tracks in the new snow. |
| Day 2 | Session 2 opens: the open investigation (part 2). If the PCs sit still, Lucan pushes: he wonders aloud about the bell tower, or the register. | |
| Day 2, evening | Severin, warm as ever, announces a vigil: tomorrow is Long Night, and the merchant will be honoured. | Ansel can't hide his jealousy. |
| Night 2 | The widow goes down to rest. She'd been asking about the changed word: her death is a reaction, and a clue. | |
| Day 3 | Her knitting is left on the refectory bench, unfinished. The investigation goes on. If the PCs still sit still, Lucan goes digging alone and nearly gets caught; the PCs cover for him or not. | |
| End of session 2 (day 3 at the latest) | **The unmasking. Always happens (Ant).** By the PCs' own route if they've gathered enough; if not, Lucan decides it is enough, tears off the hat and accuses the abbot in front of everyone. Combat opens session 3. | |
| Night 3: Long Night | Lucan was to go down to rest. Session 3: the monks and the abbot. | |

**Pulled forward:** if the PCs dig where the monks notice (the register, the
crypt door, Wren's reliquary, the blocked stair), Severin moves Lucan's turn
up to tonight. Session 2 can then end on day 2.

## 2. Session 2: the open investigation

No clue is required. Each place reveals something and points to at least two
others. The unmasking always closes session 2 (Ant); the routes decide how.

| Place or person | What's there | Points to |
|-----------------|--------------|-----------|
| **The guest register** (Hollis, guest wing) | Arrivals for forty winters. Small lamp marks beside some names, and none of those marked is ever recorded leaving. "Master Orrin Gilt" is marked, this year, first. | The crypt; Hollis; Gilt |
| **The infirmary** (Sister Wren) | Sick brothers with frost-blue lips, recovering. A reliquary of ice shards that heal. Wren's guilt, if pushed. | The crypt (where the ice comes from); the abbot |
| **The bell tower** (the dwarf stonemason) | The old stair down is blocked with newer stone. The old lamp carvings are chiselled off everywhere except over the abbot's door. From the top, steam rises from a vent in the glacier. | The chapter house; the glacier stair |
| **The blind soldier's nights** | Dragging footsteps going down. Footsteps from the merchant's room every hour, all night. | Teodor and the crypt; Gilt |
| **The crypt door** (Brother Teodor) | Sealed, rimed with frost. Inside, the newest niches are empty. Drag marks run to a chute. The young husband's boot. | The glacier; the abbot |
| **The abbot's tests** | The hymn, the lamp oil ("you'll know where"), the changed grace word. Gilt flinches or covers too smoothly. The widow noticed the grace. | Gilt; Severin knows him |
| **The relic-seller** (red herring) | Caught creeping at night: she's prising gold leaf off the chapel lamps. She saw Hollis marking the register by candlelight. | The register |
| **The boy and Ansel** | Ansel is grooming the boy to take vows. He lets slip: "The abbot never looked at me the way he looks at that merchant." | Gilt; Severin |
| **Bumping into Gilt** | A thin man inside a fat one. | Gilt |

**Routes to the unmasking:**

1. The tests, the widow and the soldier: the PCs confront Gilt, and he tells
   them, then asks for their help.
2. The register: why is a stranger marked first? Asking Hollis or Gilt opens it.
3. Fallback (Ant): at the end of session 2, if the PCs haven't gathered enough, Lucan
   reveals himself anyway and accuses the abbot. It always happens.

## 3. Session 1: the journey and the first night

1. **Strong start (Ant):** already climbing; the mule slips on the first ice
   shelf and Gilt's chest goes over the edge on its rope. Gilt shrieks more
   for the chest than the mule. (Suggested, unconfirmed: it holds Lucan's
   armour and the lamp symbol for the reveal.)
2. **The climb:** one short scene of cold, the wayside lamp shrine, and Gilt
   knowing the path.
3. **Ice Mephits (4, CR 1/2):** at the wind-scoured shrine. A quick fight;
   their death bursts are a taste of cold to come. Gilt's healing "luck".
4. **Arrival:** Hollis at the gate; warm rooms; the register signed.
5. **Supper:** meet the six pilgrims and the monks. Severin's hymn and grace.
6. **The night:** snow closes the pass; the soldier's sounds.
7. **Morning:** the young husband is gone. End.

**Every PC gets a moment:** set in prep once the PCs exist.
