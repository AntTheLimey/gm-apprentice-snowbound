# Saint Aubren's Rest (cut down into the mountain)

Named for the order's founder. "Rest" is the monks' word for carrying the
dead down to the crypt.

Ant, 2026-11-16. Each level down is colder, older and nearer the truth. The
sessions descend with it. Every level wants a player map and a GM map.

1. **Gatehouse and guest wing** (top): warm, lamplit; the pilgrims sleep
   here. Session 1.
2. **Cloister, refectory, bell tower:** daily life; the stonemason's work.
   Sessions 1-2.
3. **Chapter house and the abbot's rooms:** the old lamp symbol over the
   door; the session 3 fight.
4. **The ice crypt:** where the dead are carried "down to rest"; sealed to
   guests. Session 2's deepest reach, or session 3.
5. **The old stair into the glacier:** session 4's traps and hazards, ending
   at the dragon's lair.

Open: detailed layout per level.
