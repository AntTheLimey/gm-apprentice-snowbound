# Snowbound: shape (confirmed decisions)

- **System:** D&D 5e (2024), SRD.
- **Premise so far:** The PCs escort a wealthy pilgrim, a fat mustachioed
  merchant, up to a mountain monastery for the winter. The snow closes the
  pass. Pilgrims start dying: the whole order feeds its dead to the dragon
  asleep under the glacier, in return for the dragon's breath in the ice,
  which cures the sickness the order has carried for generations. Too few
  monks die, so the abbot chooses pilgrims from a roster, and the client is
  at the top. The client is an ex-novice come back to kill the dragon: a
  cleric of the order's own original god, disguised.
- **Core tension:** keep the client alive in a house where every friendly
  face wants him dead, while working out what the monastery really is.
- **Ending:** two fights. The abbot and the monks (the client tears off his
  disguise to confront the abbot); a short rest; the dragon. The client
  can't win alone: he supports, the PCs land the blows.
- **Scope:** four sessions.
  1. Join the merchant; the climb, with a quick ice-monster fight; settle
     in; meet the pilgrims. Ends: the first pilgrim is missing.
  2. Investigation; a second murder or disappearance. Ends: the merchant
     unmasked, a cliffhanger into the fight with the monks.
  3. The monks and the abbot; the remaining pilgrims dealt with; short rest.
  4. The dangerous approach to the lair (traps and hazards, not fights);
     the dragon.
- **Adventure shape:** fixed spine; session 2 open (investigation nodes across
  the monastery, no single clue required, several routes to the unmasking).
- **Driving forces:** Severin's clock: the dragon is fed on Long Night at
  midwinter, and Lucan is meant for it; earlier pilgrims are ordinary
  feeding; digging too close brings the date forward. If the PCs sit still,
  Lucan pushes them.
- **Party:** four PCs at level 5, plus the client as an NPC ally (cleric).
- **Monsters:** Young White Dragon (SRD, CR 6) at the end; Ice Mephits
  (CR 1/2) suggested for the fight on the climb.
- **Tone:** snowbound horror. Kind, warm monks who have all agreed to the
  killings; slow dread; watched in a house of kindness.
- **The order (rules):** Monks are Warriors of the Open Hand. The abbot and
  two or so acolytes are cold priests drawing power from the dragon (SRD
  Priest / Priest Acolyte reskinned; abbot built up as boss). The god is the
  Lantern at the Pass (travellers, winter shelter, a lit lamp); the client
  is its Life Domain cleric.
- **Rules stance:** SRD as the base; homebrew as needed (Ant).
- **Disguise:** Hat of Disguise (Disguise Self, 1 hour, renewed; fools the
  eye not the hand); the reveal is tearing off the hat.
- **Monastery:** Saint Aubren's Rest, cut down into the mountain, five levels from the guest wing
  to the glacier stair; the sessions descend with it.
- **Names:** Abbot Severin; the client is Lucan, alias Master Orrin Gilt.
- **Open:** nothing at shape level.
