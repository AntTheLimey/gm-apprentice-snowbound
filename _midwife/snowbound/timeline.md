# Severin's clock

Three days (Ant: the climb, the fight and the arrival are one day; a victim
every night). Harrowford is the valley town (confirmed).

| When | What happens | What the PCs can notice |
|------|--------------|-------------------------|
| Day 1, dawn | Master Orrin Gilt has hired the four PCs in Harrowford (the evening before). They set off up the pass. | He paid in old coin, too much of it, up front. Gilt knows the path better than a merchant should. |
| Day 1, midday | Ice Mephits at the wind-scoured shrine with the old lamp carved on it. | Gilt "prays" over a wounded PC; the wound closes. "Luck," he says. |
| Day 1, evening | Arrival at Saint Aubren's Rest. Hollis at the gate. Supper with the pilgrims. Severin's first test: he hums the old evening hymn behind Gilt's chair, and says grace with "light" changed to "breath". | Gilt goes still at the hymn. The widow frowns at the grace. |
| Night 1 | The snow closes the pass. Hollis marks the register. Teodor carries the young husband down to rest (a sleeping draught at supper). | The soldier hears dragging, and hourly footsteps from Gilt's room (the hat). |
| Day 2, morning | The young husband is gone. Hollis: "He left before dawn, poor soul. Grief makes men foolish." **Session 1 ends.** | No tracks in the new snow. |
| Day 2 | Session 2 opens: the open investigation (part 2). If the PCs sit still, Lucan pushes: he wonders aloud about the bell tower, or the register. | |
| Day 2, evening | Severin, warm as ever, announces a vigil: tomorrow is Long Night, and the merchant will be honoured. | Ansel can't hide his jealousy. |
| Night 2 | The widow goes down to rest. She'd been asking about the changed word: her death is a reaction, and a clue. | |
| Day 3 | Her knitting is left on the refectory bench, unfinished. The investigation goes on. If the PCs still sit still, Lucan goes digging alone and nearly gets caught; the PCs cover for him or not. | |
| End of session 2 (day 3 at the latest) | **The unmasking. Always happens (Ant).** By the PCs' own route if they've gathered enough; if not, Lucan decides it is enough, tears off the hat and accuses the abbot in front of everyone. Combat opens session 3. | |
| Night 3: Long Night | Lucan was to go down to rest. Session 3: the monks and the abbot. | |

**Pulled forward:** if the PCs dig where the monks notice (the register, the
crypt door, Wren's reliquary, the blocked stair), Severin moves Lucan's turn
up to tonight. Session 2 can then end on day 2.
