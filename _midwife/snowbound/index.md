## Seed

A mountain monastery shut in for the winter with a dozen pilgrims, a murder on the first night, and the monks' secret: they have been bargaining with the dragon asleep under the glacier. (Closed-room mystery; everyone a suspect; one big monster at the end.)

## Active thread

Bargain: the monks give the dragon their dead; it gives the cure (breath in the ice heals the order's old sickness; without it monks die in spring). Pilgrim murders are payment. Everyone knows; the abbot chooses who dies (a duty roster). PCs are hired escorts for a wealthy pilgrim the abbot has already picked for the roster. Client: the pilgrim who came back (ex-novice, disguised as a fat merchant, here to kill the dragon; transforms into a shining hero at the showdown). He can't win alone: a cleric, support; the PCs land the blows. Serves the order's original god. Ending: abbot and monks fight, short rest, dragon. Scope: four sessions (arrival to first missing pilgrim; investigation to unmasking; monks and abbot plus short rest; approach and dragon). Party: four PCs at level 5 plus the client as ally; Young White Dragon; session 4 approach is traps and hazards. Tone: snowbound horror (kind monks, all complicit).
