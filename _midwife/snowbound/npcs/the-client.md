# Lucan (alias Master Orrin Gilt)

- Hired the PCs to escort him up to the monastery and down in spring.
- Cover: a fat, mustachioed merchant. Charming and difficult.
- Truth: twenty years ago a novice here who ran away; knows the bargain
  (the dead for the dragon's cure). Came back to kill the dragon.
- Has powers now that will help the group in the final fight; hides them
  under a disguise.
- The PCs may get chances to notice he is not all he seems.
- Showdown: rips off the disguise to confront the abbot, transforming into a
  shining hero; then (presumably) the dragon.
- The abbot has put him at the top of the roster. The abbot was his teacher
  and saw through the disguise on the first evening (see the-abbot.md).
- He can't win alone: a support class, a cleric presumably. His power makes
  the final fight winnable; the PCs land the blows.
- Serves the order's own original god; the hero wears its old symbol,
  which still hangs unrecognised over the abbot's door.
- God: the Lantern at the Pass; he is its Life Domain cleric.
- Disguise: Hat of Disguise. Recasts Disguise Self hourly (slips away; wakes
  at night); a touch feels the thin man inside. Reveal: tears off the hat.
- Open: nothing at shape level.
