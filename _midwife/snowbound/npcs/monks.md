# The monks of Saint Aubren's Rest

Ant, 2026-11-16: go with these; there must be two cold acolytes. The rank and
file are Monks (Warriors of the Open Hand). The abbot (the-abbot.md) and two
acolytes are cold priests, their power from the dragon.

1. **Brother Hollis, the guestmaster. Cold acolyte.** First friendly face:
   soup, blankets, the best room for the merchant. Keeps the guest register,
   and the roster is written in it among the arrivals. A PC reading over his
   shoulder is closer to the truth than they know.
2. **Brother Ansel, the precentor. Cold acolyte.** Leads the singing; young,
   devout, the abbot's favourite since the novice ran away: the pupil who
   stayed. Quietly jealous of the merchant the abbot keeps testing.
3. **Sister Wren, the infirmarian. Monk.** Nurses the sick brothers; most
   worn down; believes in the cure and hates its cost. Might crack if pushed;
   whether anyone pushes is the players' call.
4. **Brother Teodor, the bearer. Monk.** Huge, gentle, near-silent; carries
   the dead "down to rest". The heavy dragging the blind soldier hears. In
   session 3, the Monk in the doorway.

Plus unnamed Open Hand brothers and sisters for the session 3 fight
(number set in prep).
