# The pilgrims (all innocent)

Kept as offered (Ant, 2026-11-16). None of them matters to the story except
as innocents caught in the grinder. They need fleshing out for colour later.
Whether the PCs care about them is the players' call.

1. **The widow.** Old, knits, praying for her late husband; knows every hymn,
   so might notice the abbot's changed word in grace.
2. **The young husband.** Wife sick in the valley; begging the god to save her.
3. **The dwarf stonemason.** Mending the bell tower as penance; notices the
   building: the old symbol over the abbot's door, blocked passages, newer
   stone where there shouldn't be any.
4. **The boy who wants to join.** About fifteen, adores the monks; left alone,
   he becomes one of them.
5. **The halfling relic-seller.** Cheerful, sells fake saints' bones; hiding
   something, just not that (red herring).
6. **The blind old soldier.** Sleeps badly; hears footsteps in the cloister
   at night and something heavy dragged.

Open: names; who dies first and second (can be left to session prep).
