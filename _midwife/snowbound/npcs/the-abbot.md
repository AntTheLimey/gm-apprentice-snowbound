# Abbot Severin

- **Role:** head of the order; writes the roster; villain of session 3.
- **Truth:** the client's old teacher. He trained the novice who ran away
  twenty years ago. He recognised him through the disguise on the first
  evening and put him at the top of the roster, out of grief at being
  betrayed.
- **Session 3:** a reckoning between teacher and pupil.
- **Clue for the PCs:** why would the abbot pick a merchant he's never met?
- **Manner:** he tests him. Warm and courteous in public, dropping things
  only a former novice would catch: hums the old evening hymn as he passes;
  "fetch the lamp oil, you'll know where"; says grace with one word changed.
  The merchant flinches or covers too smoothly. Clues to both men at once.
- **Rules:** cold priest, power from the dragon (SRD Priest reskinned to
  cold, built up as session 3's boss), with two or so cold acolytes.
- **Open:** how far the sickness has him.
