# Discoveries

## 2026-11-16: three opening seeds offered (Ant asked for seeds)

1. **The Bridge Town.** A town built along one enormous old bridge, where
   the Toll Wardens above and the barge-folk living in the arches below are
   one bad night from open war, and the PCs have just been hired by both.
   (Two levels of the same place; many NPCs; easy reason to split.)
2. **The Last Week of the Fair.** A travelling fair's owner has died owing a
   debt to something that isn't human, and it comes to collect a piece of
   the fair each night until the debt is paid or the fair closes. (Hub with
   stalls and acts; a fight most nights; a ticking clock.)
3. **Snowbound.** A mountain monastery shut in for the winter with a dozen
   pilgrims, a murder on the first night, and the monks' secret: they have
   been bargaining with the dragon asleep under the glacier. (Closed-room
   mystery; everyone a suspect; one big monster at the end.)

## 2026-11-16: the bargain (Ant)

The monks give the dragon their dead. That is why the pilgrims are being
murdered: someone inside the monastery is making dead to pay it.
(Offered alongside, not chosen: "they give it names", "they give it nothing
yet, one life freely given". Their parts are free to reuse.)

## 2026-11-16: what the dragon gives (Ant)

The cure. The dragon's breath, taken from the ice in small amounts, heals a
sickness the order has carried for generations; without it the monks start
dying in spring. (Offered, not chosen: the pass, closed all winter against
something on the other side; the cold, its sleep holding the glacier still.)

## 2026-11-16: who knows (Ant)

Everyone knows, and the abbot chooses. The whole order is complicit; pilgrims
are taken in each autumn for this purpose; the murders are a duty roster.
The PCs are guests in a house where every friendly face is in on it.
(Offered, not chosen: the abbot refuses and a faction pays behind his back;
nobody knows, a lone infirmarian kills out of love.)

## 2026-11-16: why the PCs are there (Ant)

Escorts. Hired to bring a wealthy pilgrim up the mountain and back down in
spring. The client is charming and difficult, and the abbot has already
picked them for the roster: the PCs' job and pay depend on keeping alive the
one person the whole house wants dead. (Offered, not chosen: the PCs are
pilgrims themselves on the roster; looking for someone who never came back
last winter. The second was offered as combinable; still free to add.)

## 2026-11-16: the client (Ant)

The pilgrim who came back. Made this climb twenty years ago as a novice and
ran away; knows the bargain. He has come back to kill the dragon. He has
powers now that will help the group in the final fight. He keeps himself
disguised: a fat, mustachioed merchant. The PCs may get chances to notice he
is not all he seems. At the showdown with the abbot (and then, presumably,
the dragon) he rips off the disguise to confront the abbot, transforming from
the fat merchant into a shining hero ready to fight. The abbot recognised him
on the first evening (from the seed; unconfirmed whether the abbot sees
through the disguise).
(Offered, not chosen: the heir, paid for by family in the valley; the old
soldier with the order's cough.)

## 2026-11-16: what he can't do without the PCs (Ant)

He can't win alone. He's a support class, a cleric presumably: his power can
break the dragon's hold or protect, but someone else has to land the blows.
The PCs win the fight; he makes it winnable. (Offered, not chosen: he can't
get close, the abbot watches him; he can't be trusted alone, twenty years of
hate.)

## 2026-11-16: his god, and the ending (Ant)

He serves the order's own original god. The monastery was built for that god
(mercy or dawn, to be named); the bargain betrays it. He ran away to find
the god the order abandoned and comes back as its answer. On the reveal the
hero wears the order's original symbol, which still hangs, unrecognised,
over the abbot's door. (Offered, not chosen: a god of the dead; a god of
light against the cold.)

Ending: two big fights. First the showdown with the abbot and the monks;
then a short rest; then the dragon.

## 2026-11-16: scope, four sessions (Ant)

1. Join the merchant and take him up to the monastery, with a short, quick
   fight against an ice monster on the way. Settle in, meet the other
   pilgrims. Ends when the first pilgrim is found missing.
2. Investigation. A second murder or disappearance. Ends on the merchant
   unmasked, a cliffhanger going into the fight with the monks.
3. The fight with the monks and the abbot. The remaining pilgrims get dealt
   with somehow. A short rest before the dragon.
4. The dangerous approach to the dragon's lair, then the dragon fight.
(Offered, not chosen: one long one-shot; six to eight sessions.)
Party level not yet set (Prentice suggested 5, for a young white dragon).

## 2026-11-16: party, dragon, the approach (Ant)

Four PCs at level 5, plus the client (the merchant-monk cleric) as an NPC
ally. The dragon is the SRD Young White Dragon (CR 6). The climb's quick
fight: Ice Mephits (CR 1/2) suggested. Session 4's approach is traps and
hazards, not fights, so the party reaches the dragon on one short rest
without being bled dry.
(Offered, not chosen: three PCs at 5; five PCs at about 7 with an adult dragon.)

## 2026-11-16: tone (Ant)

Snowbound horror. The monks are kind, warm, hospitable, and every one of
them has agreed to the killings. Slow dread: the one who pours your soup
might carry the roster to your door. Being watched in a house of kindness.
(Offered, not chosen: heroic adventure with a dark secret; grim and wintry
moral grey.)

## 2026-11-16: the abbot (Ant)

The client's old teacher. He trained the novice who ran away twenty years
ago, recognised him on the first evening through the disguise, and put him
top of the roster: grief at being betrayed. Session 3 is a reckoning between
the two. A clue for the PCs: why would the abbot pick a merchant he's never
met? (Offered, not chosen: the gentle father; the dying abbot. Prentice
suggested combining gentle father with teacher; Ant chose teacher alone, so
his manner is still open.)

## 2026-11-16: the abbot's manner (Ant)

He tests him. Warm and courteous in public, but keeps dropping things only a
former novice would catch: humming the old evening hymn as he passes, asking
the merchant to "fetch the lamp oil, you'll know where", saying grace with
one word changed. The merchant flinches or covers too smoothly. Sharp PCs
read both men from the same moments: who the merchant is, and that the abbot
knows. Clue engine for session 2.
(Offered, not chosen: warm and attentive; avoids him.)

## 2026-11-16: the pilgrims (Ant)

All innocent pilgrims. Nobody among the guests is in on the bargain.
Whether the PCs care about, protect or abandon the remaining pilgrims in
session 3 is up to the players (Ant, correcting my "someone to protect"
framing). Prep sets out where the pilgrims are and what happens to them if
nobody acts; it never assumes the PCs will help. Whether they carry harmless secrets (red
herrings for session 2) is still open.
(Offered, not chosen: some in on it; classic whodunit with secrets; they're
your responsibility, which is the nearest to Ant's answer.)

## 2026-11-16: the god, and the rules (Ant)

The Lantern at the Pass is fine as background (god of travellers and shelter
in winter; symbol a lit lamp). Ant: the god, its clerics and its fallen
clerics must fit D&D rules, and the order is literally monks.
Rules facts checked (SRD 5.2): cleric subclass in the SRD is Life Domain
only; Monk subclass is Warrior of the Open Hand; Oathbreaker is not in the
SRD (and is a paladin thing). No monk NPC stat block in the SRD: Priest
Acolyte (CR 1/4), Priest (CR 2), Cultist (1/8), Cultist Fanatic (2), Warrior
Veteran (3) exist; fighting monks need stat blocks built from Monk class
features. The Lantern fits Life Domain (preserving life, shelter).
Next: where the order's power comes from now (offered).

## 2026-11-16: where the order's power comes from (Ant)

- The monks are Monks, Warriors of the Open Hand (power from discipline).
- The abbot and a couple of acolytes are cold priests: their power comes from
  the dragon now. Built as SRD Priest / Priest Acolyte reskinned to cold.
  (The SRD Priest is CR 2; the abbot will need building up as session 3's
  boss.)
- The client is a Life Domain cleric of the Lantern at the Pass.
(Offered, not chosen as given: god withdrew and no casters; all dragon's
priests; god still answers. Ant mixed them.)
Next asked: how the disguise works (SRD checked: Hat of Disguise, uncommon,
attunement, casts Disguise Self, which lasts 1 hour; Disguise Kit 25 GP).

## 2026-11-16: the disguise; homebrew (Ant)

Hat of Disguise (SRD). Tells from the rules: Disguise Self lasts an hour, so
he slips away to renew it and wakes hourly at night (the blind soldier hears);
it fools the eye, not the hand (bump into him, feel a thin man). Reveal: he
tears off the hat. The hat may become loot or an early clue.
Campaign principle (Ant): "We homebrew as we need to." SRD is the base, not
a cage.
