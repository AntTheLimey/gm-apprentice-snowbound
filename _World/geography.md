## Overview

[[Harrowford]] is the last valley town. From it, [[The Pass]] climbs a day's
walk to [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] at its head, above a glacier.

## Details

- The monastery is cut down into the mountain, level below level.

## Cross-Domain Links

- Calendar: the pass closes for winter.

## GM Notes

The old stair runs from under the ice crypt down into the glacier, to the
dragon's lair.
