## Overview

**Long Night** is the longest night of the year, at midwinter, kept with a
vigil at [[Saint Aubren's Rest]].

## Details

- Snow closes [[The Pass]] at the start of winter; travellers caught above it
  winter at the monastery until spring.

## Cross-Domain Links

- Religion: the Lantern's vigil.

## GM Notes

Long Night is when the dragon is fed. This year the order means it to be the
merchant.
