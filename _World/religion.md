## Overview

[[The Lantern at the Pass]] is a god of travellers and of shelter in winter.
Its symbol is a lit lamp. Its clerics keep people alive (Life Domain).
[[The Order of Saint Aubren]] was founded under it as a winter refuge.

## Details

- Wayside shrines on the pass carry the lamp.

## Cross-Domain Links

- Calendar: Long Night.

## GM Notes

The order has betrayed the Lantern for the dragon's bargain and chiselled its
carvings off, except the symbol over the abbot's door. The abbot and two
acolytes draw cold power from the dragon. The Lantern's only cleric in the
story is Lucan.
