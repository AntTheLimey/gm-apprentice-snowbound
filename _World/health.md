## Overview

The monks of [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] carry an old sickness that leaves the
lips frost-blue. [[Sister Wren]] nurses the sick brothers in the infirmary.

## Details

## Cross-Domain Links

- Religion: the order.

## GM Notes

Without the dragon's breath in the ice, sick monks die in spring. Shards of
that ice, kept in a reliquary in the infirmary, heal them. The order has
carried the sickness for generations.

It is catching, slowly, through nursing. [[Brother Hollis]] carried it down to the valley in late summer, lodging with Tam and Hesta Rowe while collecting alms; Hesta nursed him and caught it, and it was Hollis who told Tam the house could heal her. Hesta will die in spring without the breath. [[Grenna Fallowmere]] came up the pass to learn the cure. (GM ruling, 2026-11-18.)

**Where it comes from** (GM ruling, 2026-11-18): the dragon's own cold, rising through the ice. It makes the sickness and sells the cure; the house stays sick so the house stays fed. Hesta's case is the same sickness.

**If the dragon dies** (GM ruling, 2026-11-18): its cold stops rising and nobody new sickens. The already sick get only the shards left in Wren's reliquary (six; three cure one person over three nights); the rest die in spring. Hesta is one of the already sick.

**Who is sick now** (GM ruling, 2026-11-18, for session 4): four people. [[Brother Hollis]] and two older brothers in the infirmary, and Hesta Rowe in the valley. Six shards are left: three with [[Sister Wren]] and three in [[Grenna Fallowmere]]'s coat. That cures two of the four. A shard pressed on someone heals 2d4 + 3, and each one spent that way is a third of someone's cure.
