## Description

A top-down battle map of the wayside shrine on the pass, one square to five feet: the path, the shrine, the drifts behind it and the drop beside the road.

## The Text

- The path climbs south to north across the map, about fifteen feet wide.
- The shrine stands on the uphill side of the path: a squat stone hood over a carved lamp, open at the front.
- Snowdrifts pile behind and beside the shrine, waist-high to chest-high.
- On the downhill side the path ends at a sheer drop.
- A few boulders and a fallen cairn give cover.
