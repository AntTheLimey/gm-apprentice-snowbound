## Description

A player's map of the road from Harrowford up to Saint Aubren's Rest: the switchbacks, the ice shelves and the wayside shrine.

## The Text

- Harrowford at the foot.
- The ice shelves, about a third of the way up.
- The long switchbacks, and the steep Widow's Stair cutting across them.
- The wayside shrine at the halfway point.
- Saint Aubren's Rest at the head of the pass.
