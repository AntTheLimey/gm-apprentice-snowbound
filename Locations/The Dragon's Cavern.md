## Description

The dragon's cavern under the glacier, below [[The Ice Cave]]: blue ice, forty winters of coin frozen into the floor, the dead standing in the walls, and meltwater under thin ice along the foot of the glacier wall.

## What's Here

- The dead in the walls, the order's and the pilgrims', each with the register's little lamp scratched on a tag at the collar.
- The hoard: about 2,400 gp in old coin frozen into the floor, and a dozen garnets and moonstones worth about 600 gp. The party took it.
- The body of [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]], in the meltwater ten feet short of the glacier wall.
