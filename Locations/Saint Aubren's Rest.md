## Description

A monastery cut down into the mountain at the head of the pass, its lamps the
last light a traveller sees before the snow. Bells, woodsmoke, the smell of
barley soup.

## What's Here

- **The levels:** the guest wing at the top, then the cloister, the chapter
  house, and the ice crypt below.

## History

Named for the order's founder, Saint Aubren. Built as a winter refuge for
travellers, under the Lantern at the Pass. The monks call carrying their dead
down to the crypt "going down to rest".
