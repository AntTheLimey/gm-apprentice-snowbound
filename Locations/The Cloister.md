## Description

The second level: cloister, refectory, infirmary and the bell tower. The monks' daily life.

## What's Here

- **The refectory:** supper with the pilgrims and the monks; the abbot says grace.
- **The infirmary:** Sister Wren's sick brothers.
- **The bell tower:** under repair by the stonemason.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — part of it

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Supper in the refectory: the abbot's grace, Ilyra's silver lamp. At breakfast Tam's place was empty.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — The bell tower's lamp carvings are chiselled off but for the one over the abbot's door; steam rises from the glacier below the chapter house. The office for Brother Matthias was sung in the chapel with the old hymn.
