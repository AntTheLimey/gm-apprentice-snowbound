## Description

The fourth level: where the order's dead are carried down to rest. Sealed to guests.

## What's Here

- **The crypt door:** sealed and rimed with frost; Brother Teodor keeps it.
- **Inside:** the newest niches stand empty, drag marks run to a chute polished like glass, and a cold wind breathes up it. The chute drops to [[The Ice Cave]].
