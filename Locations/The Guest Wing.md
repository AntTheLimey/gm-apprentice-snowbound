## Description

The top level: gatehouse and guest wing. Warm rooms, thick blankets, a lamp in every window.

## What's Here

- **The gatehouse:** where Brother Hollis meets every arrival.
- **The guest register:** forty winters of arrivals, kept by Hollis.
- **The pilgrims' rooms:** where the six pilgrims, the merchant and the party sleep.
