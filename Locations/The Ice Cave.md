## Description

A cave in the glacier at the foot of the crypt chute: the order's dead laid in rows in the ice, and the pilgrims with them. A pool of meltwater lies across the way on under a skin of thin ice; beyond it a slope of slippery ice runs down to the dragon's cavern.

## What's Here

- [[Tam Rowe]], frozen at the foot of the chute, in his shirt, with his boot beside him.
- The order's dead, and the pilgrims, in rows in the ice.
