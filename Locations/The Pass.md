## Description

The road from Harrowford up to [[Saint Aubren's Rest]]: switchbacks, ice shelves, and the
wind. One day's climb; the snow closes it for the winter.

## What's Here

- **The ice shelves:** where the mule loses its footing.
- **The wayside shrine:** wind-scoured, with the old lamp carved on it.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Harrowford]] — at the foot; [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — at the head
- **Things:** [[Map of the Pass]] — the players' map

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — The mule slipped on the ice shelf and Gilt's chest fell to a ledge; Kael took the Widow's Stair; four ice mephits at the wayside shrine. Snow closed it on night 1: Kael was turned back a hundred yards below the gate.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Kael took Grenna down at the first break in the weather, a month early, four days. It opened in spring and everyone else walked down.
