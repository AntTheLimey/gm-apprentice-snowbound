# Snowbound

## Premise

The party are hired in [[Harrowford]] to escort [[Master Orrin Gilt]], a fat,
fussy cloth merchant, up [[The Pass]] to [[Saint Aubren's Rest]], a mountain
monastery where he means to winter in prayer. The monks are the kindest
people the party has ever met. The snow closes the pass behind them, and the
pilgrims start to disappear.

## Tone & Genre

Snowbound horror in a D&D world. Lamplight, barley soup, bells and hymns; the
cold outside and the colder levels below. Kind faces, slow dread, the feeling
of being watched in a house where everyone is warm.

## Adventure Shape

A fixed spine with an open middle. Sessions 1, 3 and 4 run in order: the
climb and the first night; the fight with the monks; the descent and the
dragon. Session 2 is an open investigation across the monastery: the players
choose where to look, no single clue is required, and several routes lead to
the same cliffhanger.

## Core Tension

Keep the client alive in a house where every friendly face may want him dead,
while working out what the monastery really is.

## Key NPCs

- **[[Master Orrin Gilt]]:** the client; pays well, fusses over his chest.
  First appears on the climb.
- **[[Abbot Severin]]:** the abbot; warm, courteous, hums the old evening
  hymn. First appears at supper.
- **[[Brother Hollis]]:** the guestmaster and keeper of the register. Meets
  the party at the gate.
- **[[Brother Ansel]]:** the precentor; the abbot's favourite.
- **[[Sister Wren]]:** the infirmarian, worn down by the sick brothers.
- **[[Brother Teodor]]:** the bearer; huge, gentle, almost silent.
- **The pilgrims:** [[Marda Vell]], [[Tam Rowe]], [[Durgan Flintmere]],
  [[Pip Callow]], [[Rosie Tallowby]] and [[Aldous Crane]].

## Locations

- **[[Harrowford]]:** the last valley town.
- **[[The Pass]]:** one day's climb; ice shelves and a wayside shrine.
- **[[Saint Aubren's Rest]]:** cut down into the mountain.
  [[The Guest Wing]], [[The Cloister]], [[The Chapter House]], [[The Ice Crypt]].

## Entry Points

- The hire: Gilt pays the party, in old coin, to see him up the pass.
- The pilgrims: a young husband vanishes the first night, and the monks say
  he walked down the mountain.
- The abbot: something about the way he looks at the party's client.

## Escalation

Someone disappears every night. The abbot announces a vigil for Long Night,
with the merchant as its guest of honour. Digging where the monks notice
brings that night closer.

## CATS Pitch

- **Concept:** escort a merchant to a mountain monastery and keep him alive
  through a snowbound week.
- **Aim:** find out what is happening to the pilgrims, and survive it.
- **Tone:** slow-burn horror; kind hosts, cold dread.
- **Subject:** hospitality, faith gone wrong, people disappearing; one
  big monster.

## Session 0 Agenda

- [ ] Four PCs at level 5, D&D 5e (2024): hired swords, so give each a reason
      to take a winter job.
- [ ] One tie per PC to the client, or to one of the pilgrims.
- [ ] Lines and veils for horror: people go missing, bodies, faith betrayed.
- [ ] The client travels with the party as an NPC ally.

## GM Notes

### Driving Forces

- **[[The Order of Saint Aubren]]:** feeds its dead to
  [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]] in return for its breath in the ice, which cures the order's
  generational sickness. Too few monks die, so [[Abbot Severin]] chooses
  pilgrims from a roster written into the guest register. Every monk knows.
  Victory state: Lucan goes down to rest on Long Night and the order lives
  another winter.
- **Lucan** (the client, [[Master Orrin Gilt]]): a novice who ran away twenty
  years ago, back as a Life Domain cleric of [[The Lantern at the Pass]], the
  order's original god, disguised by a [[Hat of Disguise]], to kill the
  dragon. He can't win alone. If the PCs sit still, he pushes them.
- **[[Abbot Severin]]:** Lucan's old teacher. He saw through the disguise on
  the first evening and put him top of the roster. He tests him in public
  (the hymn, the lamp oil, grace with "light" changed to "breath").

### If They Do Nothing

Night 1, [[Tam Rowe]] goes down to rest. Night 2, [[Marda Vell]]. Night 3 is
Long Night: the merchant. At the end of session 2, if the PCs haven't
gathered enough, Lucan reveals himself anyway and accuses the abbot; session
3 opens in combat. See [[Severins_Clock]] and [[Investigation_Design]].
