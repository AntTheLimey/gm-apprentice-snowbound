## Overview

A boy of about fifteen who wants to join the order. He worships the monks, and they are kind to him.

## Appearance & Manner

Thin, red-eared and too tall for his coat, with a borrowed rope belt tied the way the monks tie theirs.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — wintering there as a pilgrim

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Sat beside Brother Ansel at supper, copying how the monks hold their bowls.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Held Ansel's music in the chapel.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Came up the stair behind the brothers with a lamp. Oswin told him the truth; he stays with Marda and keeps a lamp lit.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Ran past Brannoc to Oswin at dawn.

## GM Notes

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Breathless, eager, wants to be useful.
- **Tell:** Copies the monks' bows, slightly wrong.
- **Line:** "Brother Ansel says I could take vows by spring!"

### Wants

To be accepted into the order.

### Secrets

An innocent pilgrim, caught in the grinder; not in on the bargain. Whether
the PCs care about them is the players' call. [[Brother Ansel]] is grooming him to take vows. Left alone, he grows up to be one of them.

### Stats

**Stat block:** Commoner (SRD).
