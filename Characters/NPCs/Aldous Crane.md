## Overview

A blind old soldier who sleeps badly.

## Appearance & Manner

Big-shouldered gone thin, with a white cropped beard and eyes filmed over. He sits square to the fire with his hands on his knees, the way a sergeant sits on a bench outside a tent.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — wintering there as a pilgrim

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Knew Brannoc by his voice at the guest wing fire; had him describe the faces round the supper table.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Reported to Brannoc: dragging past his door on night 1, and footsteps from the merchant's room every hour, "a fat man who walks like a thin one". Agreed to keep listening; watched the unmasking from his doorway.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Came out blind at Brannoc's whistle and fought at the sound of habits; holds the guest wing for Brannoc: "Hale. Report."
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Waited at the head of the guest stair with his sword across his knees: "Hale. Report." "Good report." Walked down with Brannoc in spring.

## GM Notes

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Gravel and drill-square; still gives orders by habit.
- **Tell:** Turns his head towards sounds, not faces.
- **Line:** "I know that step. Hale, is that you?"

### Wants

Peace and quiet, and a night's sleep he never gets.

### Secrets

An innocent pilgrim, caught in the grinder; not in on the bargain. Whether
the PCs care about them is the players' call. He hears things at night: something heavy dragged down the stairs, and footsteps from the merchant's room every hour, all night (Gilt renewing the disguise). A witness, if anyone believes him.

### Stats

**Stat block:** Warrior Veteran (SRD), Blinded.
