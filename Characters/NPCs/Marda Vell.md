## Overview

An old widow come to pray for her late husband. She knits through every meal and knows every hymn by heart.

## Appearance & Manner

Small and stooped, in a black shawl, with grey wool and needles always in her lap and her lips moving with every hymn.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — wintering there as a pilgrim

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Said "light" under her breath when the abbot said "breath" in grace.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Sang "light" out loud at the office where the brothers sang "breath". Watched the unmasking from her doorway.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Knelt by the fallen Lucan and sang the hymn with "light"; sang it with him again under the uncut lamp. Pip stays with her.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Kept the fire all night; began cutting the whole lamp back into the stone with Lucan and Durgan.

## GM Notes

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Brisk, chatty, a little deaf; talks to her knitting.
- **Tell:** Mouths the words of every hymn a beat early.
- **Line:** "Sixty winters I've said that grace, love."

### Wants

To pray for her late husband, Edwin, and to be left to her knitting.

### Behind the Scenes

- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Was to go down to rest on night 2; Gilt was moved up instead, so her cup was plain and she slept. She is alive.

### Secrets

An innocent pilgrim, caught in the grinder; not in on the bargain. Whether
the PCs care about them is the players' call. She frowns when the abbot changes a word in grace. She goes down to rest on night 2, because she had been asking about it: her death is a reaction, and a clue. Her knitting is left on the refectory bench, unfinished.

### Stats

**Stat block:** Commoner (SRD).
