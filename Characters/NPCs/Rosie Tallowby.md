## Overview

A cheerful halfling who sells saints' bones that are not saints' bones.

## Appearance & Manner

A round, rosy halfling in a pedlar's apron with a dozen pockets, a relic case on a strap, and a laugh you can hear across the refectory.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — wintering there as a pilgrim

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Greeted Nim loudly about her debts; took ten old coins to be quiet, bit one and announced it.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Watched the unmasking from her doorway.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Caught by Nim in the chapel with a knife and a bag of gold leaf; they settled on a third.

## GM Notes

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Loud, cheerful, salesman's patter.
- **Tell:** Pats her relic case like a pet.
- **Line:** "A knucklebone of Saint Aubren himself, and for you, cousin, half price!"

### Wants

To sell every relic in the case to the pilgrims, and to see Nim pay up.

### Behind the Scenes

- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Spent the night prising gold leaf off the chapel lamps; nobody found out.

### Secrets

An innocent pilgrim, caught in the grinder; not in on the bargain. Whether
the PCs care about them is the players' call. Red herring. Caught creeping at night: she's prising gold leaf off the chapel lamps. She saw [[Brother Hollis]] marking the register by candlelight.

### Stats

**Stat block:** Commoner (SRD), with a knife.
