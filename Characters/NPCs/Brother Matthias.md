A brother of [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] who, [[Abbot Severin]] told the guests at breakfast on day 2, died in the night after a long illness. His office is to be said that evening, and the guests are invited.

## GM Notes

### Secrets

There is no Brother Matthias. Severin invented him at breakfast on day 2 when [[Brannoc Hale]] asked which brother [[Brother Teodor]] had carried down the inner stair; the sack held [[Tam Rowe]]. The office is said over an empty shroud. Asked about Matthias, [[Sister Wren]] and [[Brother Hollis]] stumble. (GM ruling, 2026-11-17.)
