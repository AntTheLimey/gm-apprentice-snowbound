## Overview

A young husband whose wife is sick in the valley. He has come to beg the god to save her.

Kael found him dead at the foot of the crypt chute, frozen in the ice cave with the order's dead.

## Appearance & Manner

A lanky young man in a patched valley coat, with a new wedding ring he cannot stop turning.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — wintering there as a pilgrim

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Overjoyed to see Kael at the guest wing; took Hesta's salve; drank Hollis's sleeping cup and went to bed early. Gone by morning, his pack and the salve left under his bed.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — His name is in the guest register with a little lamp beside it. Lucan says he is "below, with the others".
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Kael found his boot in the crypt and his body, frozen, at the foot of the chute in the ice cave.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — His coat was in the dragon's hoard with Hesta's letter in the pocket. Kael and Oswin carried him up the chute; Kael laid him out in the chapel.

## GM Notes

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Young, anxious, earnest; talks too fast about home.
- **Tell:** Turns his wedding ring round and round.
- **Line:** "Is there news of Hesta? Tell me there's news."

### Wants

His wife Hesta healed; he'll pray all winter if that's what it takes.

### Behind the Scenes

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — **Dead.** Drugged at supper on night 1, he died in his sleep; Teodor put his body down the crypt chute, and it lies frozen at the foot with the order's older dead, kept for Long Night.

### Secrets

An innocent pilgrim, caught in the grinder; not in on the bargain. Whether
the PCs care about them is the players' call. The first to go down to rest, on night 1, drugged at supper and carried down by [[Brother Teodor]]. Hollis says he left before dawn; there are no tracks in the new snow. His boot is in the ice crypt.

**Dead.** He died in his drugged sleep on night 1. Teodor put his body down the crypt chute; it lies frozen at the foot, in the ice cave above [[The Dragon Under the Glacier]]'s lair, with the order's older dead, kept for the dragon's feeding on Long Night. The party can find it there. There is no rescue: only the body, the boot, and the news Kael carries home to Hesta.
