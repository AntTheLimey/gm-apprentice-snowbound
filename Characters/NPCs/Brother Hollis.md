## Overview

The guestmaster: the first friendly face at the gate, with soup, blankets and the best room for anyone who looks tired.

The little lamps in the guest register were his: the list of who would be carried down next. The register's Long Night page, in his hand, named the merchant "to rest" and Oswin "to carry".

## Appearance & Manner

Round-faced, never without a lamp. Writes everything in the guest register.

## Related

- **Groups:** [[The Order of Saint Aubren]] — guestmaster
- **Places:** [[The Guest Wing]] — usually found here

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Met the party at the gate with soup and blankets and had them all sign the guest register. Brought Tam a cup to help him sleep; at breakfast said Tam had left before dawn. Held the gate for Kael when he came back from the drifts.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Let Kael read the guest register at his desk: "Guests we pray for, child. Every one." Brought warm cups round the guest wing on night 2 and poured Gilt's from a jug of its own.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Dropped by Nim's first dagger over the guest register. Grenna saved his life after the fight; barred in the chapter house with the other brothers.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Read every name in the register aloud to Ilyra until noon. Died in the spring, as Wren knew he would.

### Secrets

Keeps the guest register, and the roster is written in it among the arrivals as small lamp marks. A PC reading over his shoulder is closer to the truth than they know. On the first night he marks the register. Cover story for the young husband: "He left before dawn, poor soul. Grief makes men foolish." Every monk has agreed to the killings.

In late summer he went down to the valleys for alms with the cough on him and lodged with Tam and Hesta Rowe; Hesta nursed him and caught the sickness. He told Tam the house could heal her, which is why Tam came up. (GM ruling, 2026-11-18.)
