## Overview

[[Kael Stonehand]]'s sister and [[Tam Rowe]]'s wife, in the valley below the pass. She took the house's sickness in the summer, and Tam went up to [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] to pray for her. She wrote him a letter: "If the brothers want paying, don't pay it."
