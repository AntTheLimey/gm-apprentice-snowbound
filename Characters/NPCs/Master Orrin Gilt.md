## Overview

A wealthy cloth merchant from the valley who hired the party in Harrowford to
see him up the pass to [[Saint Aubren's Rest]], where he means to spend the winter in prayer,
and back down in the spring. He pays well, in old coin, up front.

He is not a merchant. On the second night at the Rest he tore off his hat and stood as Lucan, a thin, older man in oiled mail and the white surcoat with the whole lamp, and told [[Abbot Severin]] that he had taught him the hymn.

## Appearance & Manner

Fat, florid and magnificently moustached, in furs too fine for the mountain.
Fusses over his luggage, his mule and above all his locked chest. It holds no
wool: [[Nim Underbough]] opened it and found a coat of oiled mail, a longsword
and a white surcoat with the whole lamp worked in gold thread on the breast.

## Story

### Session 3: Peace, Child

He fought beside them in the corridor, which is to say he stood where the abbot could see him and was hit for it, twice, by the cold. He went down in Gilt's doorway in the white surcoat with the whole lamp on it, and a brother he had taught the hymn to knelt over him with a mace.

He woke by the fire with a healer's potion in him and asked whether the abbot was dead. Then he told them why he ran: he was nineteen, and they told him the bargain, and asked him to carry. He looked at the novice a long time. "He was raising you to carry. As he raised me."

He lit a lamp under the carving the house had left whole, and sang the hymn with "light", and said he could not do it alone. He asked for the silver lamp and did not get it. He said the prayer over it anyway. Under the glacier the dragon told him his teacher had written him first, and named him as its price.

### Session 4: Long Night Early

He sang. That was what he had, at the end: one 3rd-level slot, the lamp-light spirits, and the evening hymn with "light", and he never stopped singing it. The breath put him on the coin at its feet, and the healer said a word and he got up. The second breath put him down again, and it took him in its jaws, gently, like a dog with a pup, and went under the ice.

He opened his eyes in the dragon's mouth. Then it let him go, and the knight hauled him out by the surcoat with the whole lamp on it. On the coin he sat down and wept, once, into his hands.

He stays. Someone has to cut the whole lamp back into the stone over the abbot's door, Father's door, and he knows how it went.

**Bonus Actions / Reactions**

**Tactics:** He supports and does not win fights alone; the PCs land the blows. He opens with *Bless* or *Spirit Guardians*, puts *Warding Bond* on the front-liner, and keeps the Bloodied up with *Healing Word* and *Preserve Life*. Against [[Abbot Severin]] he wants to stand face to face, and will take a blow meant for a PC.

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** loud, self-important, quick to complain.
- **Tell:** goes very still when he hears the old evening hymn; kneels the way
  novices are taught to.
- **Line:** "Mind the chest! Mind the chest! The mule can look after itself."

### Wants

To kill the dragon under the glacier and end the bargain. Until then, to stay
alive and unrecognised. If the PCs sit still in session 2, he pushes them:
first by wondering aloud (the bell tower, the register), then by going digging
alone and nearly getting caught.

### Knows

- **Will share:** nothing true until the unmasking, unless the PCs confront him
  with enough; then everything, and he asks for their help.
- **Hides:** who he is, why he's here, what's in the chest.

### Under Pressure

Covers too smoothly. If the PCs haven't gathered enough by the end of session 2,
he decides it is enough: tears off the hat and accuses the abbot in front of
everyone.

### Secrets

He is **Lucan**, the novice who ran away twenty years ago, back as a cleric of
the order's own original god, the Lantern at the Pass, to kill the dragon.
The merchant is a *Disguise Self* illusion from the [[Hat of Disguise]]: it
lasts an hour, so he slips away to renew it and wakes hourly at night; it
fools the eye, not the hand (bump into him and feel a thin man). [[Abbot Severin]]
was his teacher, saw through the disguise on the first evening and put him
at the top of the roster. At the reveal he tears off the hat and stands in
armour bearing the order's old lamp symbol, the one still hanging over the
abbot's door. ~~Suggested, not confirmed: his armour and the symbol are in the
chest.~~ superseded 2026-11-17

Ruled at the table (Session 1): the chest holds his coat of oiled mail, a
longsword and a white surcoat with the whole lamp worked in gold thread on the
breast. [[Nim Underbough]] has opened it and seen them, and told [[Ilyra Vessant]];
Gilt doesn't know.
