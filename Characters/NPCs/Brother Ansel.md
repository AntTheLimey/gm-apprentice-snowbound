## Overview

The precentor: he leads the singing, young and devout.

## Appearance & Manner

A fine voice, a quick smile, an eye that follows the abbot.

## Related

- **Groups:** [[The Order of Saint Aubren]] — precentor
- **Places:** [[The Cloister]] — usually found here

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Led the singing at supper; Pip sat beside him.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Rehearsed the old hymn for the office and told Ilyra the abbot never looked at him the way he looks at the merchant. Came behind the abbot to the unmasking.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Blessed the brothers and went for Lucan; dropped by Nim, raised by the abbot, and struck at Lucan as he lay. Yielded with Pip in his arms; barred in the chapter house, asking only that Pip be kept out of it.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Asked Oswin one thing when the chapter house was unbarred: "Does the Guest live?" At "No" he sat down on the floor.

**Tactics:** Goes for Lucan, out of jealousy, before anyone else. *Bless* on the brothers in round one. Keeps [[Pip Callow]] behind him; if Pip is threatened, Ansel breaks off to shield him.

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Clear, musical, eager; finishes the abbot's sentences.
- **Tell:** Glances at the abbot before he answers anything.
- **Line:** "Father Severin says the old hymns keep the cold out."

### Wants

To keep the order alive, and the bargain kept.

### Hidden Ties

- [[Abbot Severin]] — one of his two cold acolytes

### Secrets

The abbot's favourite since the novice ran away: the pupil who stayed. Quietly jealous of the merchant the abbot keeps testing, and can't hide it when the vigil is announced. Grooming the boy, [[Pip Callow]], to take vows. Lets slip: "The abbot never looked at me the way he looks at that merchant." Every monk has agreed to the killings.
