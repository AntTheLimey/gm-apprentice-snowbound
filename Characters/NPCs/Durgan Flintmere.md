## Overview

A dwarf stonemason mending the bell tower as a penance.

## Appearance & Manner

A broad, grey-bearded dwarf with mortar dust in every crease, a mason's hammer at his belt and a pencil behind his ear.

## Related

- **Places:** [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] — wintering there as a pilgrim

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Delighted Ilyra came; promised to show her the tower and sketched the refectory's carvings on the tablecloth.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Showed Ilyra the bell tower: every lamp carving chiselled off but the one over the abbot's door, and an old stair down blocked with newer stone. Watched the unmasking from his doorway.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Offered to open the old tower stair; the party took the chute.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Began cutting the whole lamp back into the stone over the abbot's door.

## GM Notes

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Gruff, proud of his craft, rumbling laugh.
- **Tell:** Raps on walls with his knuckles as he passes, listening.
- **Line:** "Someone's been at this stone, and not a mason."

### Wants

To finish the bell tower before spring, and to find out who chiselled the old carvings.

### Secrets

An innocent pilgrim, caught in the grinder; not in on the bargain. Whether
the PCs care about them is the players' call. He notices the building: the old stair down from the bell tower blocked with newer stone; the old lamp carvings chiselled off everywhere except over the abbot's door; steam rising from a vent in the glacier, seen from the top.

### Stats

**Stat block:** Tough (SRD).
