## Overview

The infirmarian: she nurses the sick brothers.

Her locked reliquary holds shards of ice that heal; three, over three nights, take the blue from a sick brother's lips. She let Grenna take three.

## Appearance & Manner

Tired eyes, cold-chapped hands, smells of camphor.

## Related

- **Groups:** [[The Order of Saint Aubren]] — infirmarian
- **Places:** [[The Cloister]] — usually found here
- **People:** [[Abbot Severin]] — serves the abbot
- **People:** [[Oswin]] — taught him the open hand

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Asked after wounds from the shrine and offered Brannoc a salve made with ice from the mountain; he declined.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Came up the inner stair with the rest of the order and sat down weeping when the abbot died. Opened the reliquary for Grenna and let her take three shards.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Asked Grenna for the shards back; chose the younger of her two sick brothers for her own three, then cried and went on working. The brothers chose her in spring.

**Traits**
- ***Focus (5 points; all return on a Short Rest).*** Fuels Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind and Stunning Strike.
- ***Open Hand Technique.*** A creature hit by her Flurry of Blows also loses its Reactions until its next turn, or is pushed 15 ft (Strength save DC 14), or is knocked Prone (Dexterity save DC 14).

**Actions**
- ***Extra Attack.*** Two Unarmed Strikes.
- ***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. Once per turn, 1 Focus: ***Stunning Strike***, Constitution save DC 14 or Stunned until her next turn (on a success, its Speed is halved and its next attack roll against it has Advantage).

**Bonus Actions / Reactions**
- ***Martial Arts.*** One Unarmed Strike.
- ***Flurry of Blows (1 Focus).*** Two Unarmed Strikes.
- ***Ice Shard (3/Day).*** She presses a shard from the reliquary to herself or a brother within 5 ft: 2d4 + 3 healing.
- ***Deflect Attacks (Reaction).*** Reduces the damage of a Bludgeoning, Piercing or Slashing attack that hits her by 1d10 + 8.

**Tactics:** Fights to keep the brothers standing, not to kill. She is the most worn down: if the abbot falls or the shards run out, she drops her hands and weeps. If a PC pushed her in session 2, she hesitates in round one.

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Low, tired, kind; never wastes a word.
- **Tell:** Rubs her chapped hands; smells of camphor.
- **Line:** "Sit by the fire. You'll want your fingers tomorrow."

### Wants

To keep the order alive, and the bargain kept.

### Secrets

The most worn down of them. She believes in the cure and hates what it costs. The infirmary holds a reliquary of ice shards that heal: the dragon's breath. She might crack if pushed; whether anyone pushes is the players' call. Every monk has agreed to the killings.
