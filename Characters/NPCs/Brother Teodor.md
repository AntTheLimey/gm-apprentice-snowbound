## Overview

The bearer: a huge, gentle, almost silent monk who looks after the crypt.

He was the one who carried the house's dead down to the crypt, and the drugged guests with them; Tam was one. He died in the fight in the guest wing corridor.

## Appearance & Manner

Enormous hands, careful steps, frost in his beard.

## Related

- **Groups:** [[The Order of Saint Aubren]] — bearer
- **Places:** [[The Ice Crypt]] — usually found here
- **People:** [[Abbot Severin]] — serves the abbot
- **People:** [[Oswin]] — sparred with him every morning

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Met by Brannoc on an inner stair after midnight with a heavy sack: a brother had died, going down to rest. Would not let Brannoc past the frosted door.
- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Kept the inner stair and would not let Brannoc pass or answer him. Stood at the back of the office. After midnight came to Gilt's door with a rope and Hollis's lamp.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Held the doorway against Brannoc; fell to Kael's arrows, and blind Crane hacked at the sound of his fall. The crypt key was on his belt.

**Skills** Athletics +6, Perception +6 · **Senses** Passive Perception 16 · **Languages** Common
**CR** 3 (XP 700; PB +3) · **Gear** a candle and a carving knife

**Traits**
- ***Focus (5 points; all return on a Short Rest).*** Fuels Flurry of Blows, Patient Defense, Step of the Wind and Stunning Strike.
- ***Open Hand Technique.*** As Sister Wren (save DC 14).
- ***The Doorway.*** While he stands in a doorway or on a stair, creatures can't move through his space, and he has Advantage on checks and saves against being moved or knocked Prone.

**Actions**
- ***Extra Attack.*** Two Unarmed Strikes.
- ***Unarmed Strike.*** *Melee Attack Roll:* +6, reach 5 ft. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) Bludgeoning damage. Once per turn, 1 Focus: ***Stunning Strike***, Constitution save DC 14.
- ***Carry Down.*** He grapples a creature (Escape DC 14) and can move at full Speed carrying it.

**Bonus Actions / Reactions**
- ***Martial Arts.*** One Unarmed Strike.
- ***Flurry of Blows (1 Focus).*** Two Unarmed Strikes.
- ***Deflect Attacks (Reaction).*** Reduces Bludgeoning, Piercing or Slashing damage by 1d10 + 7.

**Tactics:** Takes the doorway or the stair head and holds it, gently, without a word ("...Rest."). He does not chase. He stuns whoever gets past him and carries the stunned toward the crypt stair.

### Playing Them

- **Speech:** Almost silent: a nod, a shake of the head, a word at most.
- **Tell:** Ducks under every doorway; moves very gently for his size.
- **Line:** "...Rest."

### Wants

To keep the order alive, and the bargain kept.

### Secrets

He carries the dead "down to rest". He is the heavy dragging the blind soldier hears at night. On the first night he carries the young husband down, drugged at supper. Every monk has agreed to the killings.
