### Backstory

I grew up in the lanes behind Harrowford market, and I was lifting purses before I could count what was in them. My cousin Rosie taught me to run a patter and to read a mark, and for years we worked the fairs together: she sold the relics, I sold the audience on them, and sometimes someone's pocket sold itself. Then I borrowed against a good winter that didn't come, from people who do not take apologies. Rosie went up the pass to work the pilgrims, and I stayed to fence what I could and keep my head down, which stopped working. A merchant needed another pair of hands for an escort over the pass, with the pay up front and a long way from certain faces. I said yes before he finished the sentence.

### Appearance

Small even for a halfling, with dark curly hair kept short under a wool cap and a quick, forgettable face made for lying. Patched but good leather armor under a grey cloak, and rapier and daggers worn where a coat can hide them. Ink-stained fingers from forging passes, and a habit of looking at everyone's hands first.
