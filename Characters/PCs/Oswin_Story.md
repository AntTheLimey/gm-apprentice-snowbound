## Session 3 — Peace, Child

*Father, is it true. No, Father.*

He walked through the brothers, who let him, and stood between the abbot and Lucan with his lamp. "Father. Is it true?" The abbot told him to come here. He did not move. In the second round he said "No, Father," and hit the abbot, and kept hitting him, and the abbot never once raised a hand to him. He beat him down to almost nothing and knocked him flat on his own frost, and the abbot died in Grenna's moonlight looking past him at Pip.

On the Long Night page, in Hollis's hand, it said "Master Orrin Gilt, to rest. Oswin, to carry." He had copied these pages. He had drawn the lamps. He told [[Pip Callow|Pip]] the truth and left him with Marda. [[Master Orrin Gilt|Lucan]] looked at him a long time: "He was raising you to carry. As he raised me." Under the glacier the dragon offered him the house. He answered it in the office's own words. "Peace, Guest. Rest."

## Session 4 — Long Night Early

*Peace, Guest. Rest.*

He ran across the coin barefoot and could not land a blow, and then he hit it so hard it staggered. When it offered him its house, he said the office's words to it: "Peace, Guest. Rest." The second breath should have put him down. He kept his feet.

After, he walked along the wall of the dead and said the office over every one of them, with "light". One was the old cellarer who died in his first winter, who taught him the stairs. He prayed over a frozen pilgrim before Kael took his boots. When Kael slipped on the chute with Tam, Oswin took the other end, and they brought him up together.

At dawn [[Pip Callow|Pip]] ran past Brannoc to him. He unbarred [[The Chapter House|the chapter house]]. [[Brother Ansel]] asked if the Guest lived, and sat down on the floor at the answer. The brothers looked at Oswin. "I'll keep the lamp lit till spring," he said. "Then you choose an abbot who carries nobody." In spring they chose Wren.
