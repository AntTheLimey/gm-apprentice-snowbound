### Backstory

I set bones and draw fevers in the high valleys, the way my mother did, and I can read a sickness off a face across a room. In the autumn Hesta Rowe came to me with a cough and lips going blue as frost, and nothing I knew touched it. Her husband Tam said a brother from Saint Aubren's had lodged with them in the summer with the same cough, and that the monks up there carry it and live. So I went up the pass before the snow, to learn what they know. Sister Wren was glad of another pair of hands in the infirmary, and I have been nursing her sick brothers since. Tam came up after me, to pray. I eat with the sick and sleep beside them, which is why the other guests have hardly seen me.

### Appearance

A broad, grey-green orc woman with short tusks, iron-grey hair in a single plait, and forearms scarred by a lifetime of stills, kettles and frightened patients. Wears a leather jerkin under a herb-stained apron, a shield on her back she calls her tray, and a walking staff of mountain ash. Smells of camphor and smoke; the monks' infirmary smell has got into her clothes.

**Age:** 41 · **Height:** 6'2" · **Weight:** 215 lb · **Eyes:** Amber · **Hair:** Iron-grey, plaited · **Skin:** Grey-green · **Faith:** The old spirits of the high meadows

### Personality

- **Traits:** Looks at hands, lips and eyes before faces. Says what is wrong with you first. Never sits with her back to a sickbed.
- **Ideals:** A cure is a cure; what it costs is a question for later.
- **Bonds:** Hesta Rowe, her patient, whom she failed; and every patient she has not yet failed.
- **Flaws:** Will look away from a wrong if it might save a life she cares about. Keeps the hard news to herself until it is too late to soften.

## GM Notes

Hesta Rowe has the order's sickness ([[health]]). She caught it nursing [[Brother Hollis]] when he lodged with her and Tam in late summer, collecting alms; it was Hollis who told Tam the house could heal her, and Hollis who marked Tam in the register on his first night. Without the dragon's breath Hesta dies in spring, as the sick brothers would. The ice shards in Wren's reliquary would cure her. Grenna has seen the reliquary but not what is in it.
