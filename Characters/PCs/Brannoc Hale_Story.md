## Session 1 — The Climb

*Day 1 to the morning of day 2: one rope, four mephits, one sergeant found, one monk on a stair.*

Walked the rear as usual. Mid-morning the mule went over on the ice shelf and the merchant, [[Master Orrin Gilt]], took hold of Brannoc's arm and shouted for his chest. Brannoc put his weight on the rope instead. The chest came up heavy as a man.

At the wayside shrine four [[Ice Mephit|ice mephits]] came out of the drifts. Frost on him twice, claws once, and two of them burst in his face when [[Ilyra Vessant|Ilyra]]'s fire took them. Gilt knelt by him afterwards and said a word, and the cuts closed. He called it luck. Brannoc had seen army chaplains do that, and said so.

At the guest wing a voice by the fire said his name before he was through the door. [[Aldous Crane]], his old sergeant, alive and blind, still mocking the way he walks. At supper Brannoc gave him the faces round the table, one by one, the way he would have reported a column.

That night he kept the corridor out of habit. After midnight, dragging. He followed it to an inner stair and found [[Brother Teodor]] with a sack over his shoulder: a brother had died, going down to rest. Teodor would not let him past the frosted door, and did not move. At breakfast [[Tam Rowe]] was gone, and Brannoc put down his spoon and asked the whole table which brother had died. The abbot answered, smiling: [[Brother Matthias]].

## Session 2 — The Office for Brother Matthias

*A report taken, a door not passed, a watch kept, and a man he didn't see go by.*

[[Aldous Crane]] gave his report by the fire the way he used to give them on the line: one item, then the next. Something heavy dragged past his door on the first night, going down. Footsteps from the merchant's room every hour, all night. "A fat man who walks like a thin one." Brannoc took it as he would have taken a picket's report, and asked the old man to keep listening.

At lunch he wanted to go to the abbot and put the question straight, as he had at breakfast. He was outvoted. In the afternoon he went down the inner stair with [[Kael Stonehand|Kael]] and found [[Brother Teodor]] on the bottom step, carving a candle. Brannoc asked to pass. Teodor stood, and did not move, and did not answer.

At the office he stood beside the merchant and saw nothing he could name. That night he did not drink his cup and took the watch, and Teodor walked past him anyway. When the shout came, Brannoc was first into the corridor, and the merchant he had carried up the mountain took off his hat and was someone else.

## Session 3 — Peace, Child

*One whistle, four strokes, two breaths of cold, and a company of seven going down a hole.*

He blew the old company whistle before anyone else had drawn, and went at [[Brother Teodor]] in the doorway. Four strokes; three landed. Down the corridor a door banged and [[Aldous Crane]] came out with his greatsword, blind, swinging at habits. When Teodor fell, Crane hacked at the sound of it. Brannoc did not stop him.

The abbot said his name from the register and Brannoc was afraid of an old man in a habit, which he did not like. The second breath of cold put him on the floor. [[Grenna Fallowmere|Grenna]] got him up. Afterwards he had the brothers walked to the chapter house and barred in, and gave Crane the guest wing: "Hale. Report." He put on [[The Abbot's Ring|the abbot's ring]]. On the stair down he said the thing he had been thinking since the hymn: "We go together or not at all." Under the glacier the dragon offered him terms for the guests, if he left it the priest. "My company doesn't leave anyone."

## Session 4 — Long Night Early

*Seven went down. Seven came up, and one carried.*

He walked out across the coin to its foreleg and hit it three times. "Shields. On me." It raked him with a claw, and [[The Abbot's Ring|the abbot's ring]] took the cold out of the wound. When the priest went down he drank his potion and came back across the coin to stand over him. When the dragon bargained, bleeding, he told it terms were for armies. "You ate a boy in his shirt."

It took [[Master Orrin Gilt|Lucan]] under the ice. When [[Kael Stonehand|Kael]] killed it, Brannoc waded into the meltwater to his waist and hauled the priest out by the surcoat. In the hoard he worked [[Frost Brand|a frost-white longsword]] out of the ice.

At dawn [[Aldous Crane|Crane]] was at the head of the guest stair with his sword across his knees. "Hale. Report." "Dragon dead. Priest alive. One recovered, deceased." "Good report." In spring the two of them walked down together.
