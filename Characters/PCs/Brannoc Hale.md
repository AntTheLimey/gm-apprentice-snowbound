### Backstory

I joined the Harrow March border company at nineteen and spent nineteen years in it, the last six as a sergeant. Aldous Crane was the sergeant who taught me the job: how to walk the back of a column, how to count heads twice, how to tell a frightened recruit from a coward. The company was disbanded when the crown stopped paying for the watchtowers. I lost track of Aldous in the break-up and haven't had a word from him in two winters. Since then I have guarded wagons for people who paid late. Master Gilt paid half up front for one winter escort over the pass, and half up front is more than anyone else offered, so here I am.

### Appearance

A broad, weathered man in a dented chain hauberk that has been mended more than once, with a plain round shield on his back and a longsword that has seen more sharpening than fighting. Close-cropped grey-brown hair, a nose broken once and set badly, and the permanent squint of someone who has watched a lot of horizons.

### Personality

- **Traits:** Counts heads twice. Dry humour, mostly kept for the dark moments. Takes the rear of every column without being asked.
