### Backstory

I scouted for the army for eight years, ahead of the column, reading the ground and counting what waited in it. When the war wound down I went home to the high valleys and started guiding people over the pass for money, because I knew it better than anyone and nobody else wanted the job in winter. My sister Hesta married Tam Rowe, a gentle young man with more faith than sense. She has been ill since autumn and no healer in the valley can say why. Tam went up to the monastery to pray for her. I took Master Gilt's job as guide because it goes the same way, and because I would rather watch over Tam myself than wait for news.

### Appearance

Seven and a half feet of grey-skinned mountain stone, with the pale, swirling tattoos of a Highvale family across his shoulders and forearms. Cropped dark hair, a heavy brow, and a calm, slow stare. He wears studded leather and a long sheepskin coat, with a longbow he can draw one-handed and two shortswords at his belt.

### Personality

- **Traits:** Talks little and listens more. Reads weather and tracks before he reads faces. Quiet laugh.
