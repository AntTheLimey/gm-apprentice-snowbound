## Session 1 — The Climb

*A heavy chest, a cheap cousin, and a good lock.*

When the merchant's chest went over the ice shelf, Nim went down after it on her toes, tied a new line, and heard it settle inside: metal on metal. Not wool. She ran her thumb over the lock on the way past. A good lock. The Widow's Stair nearly finished her; she got to the top grey and shaking.

At the shrine she found the offering niche under the snow (potions, old coin, a silver lamp) and took the frost-blue bottle. At the gate she signed the register as Nell Underhill. Her cousin [[Rosie Tallowby]] saw her across the guest wing, shouted "Nimmy!" and asked about the debts at the top of her voice. Ten old coins to shut her up. Rosie bit one and announced it.

That night the chest was in a storeroom, too heavy to carry upstairs. Nim had it open in no time: under the folded wool, oiled mail, a longsword, and a white surcoat with a gold lamp on the breast, the whole one, like the shrine. A candle on the stair, and she was behind the flour sacks. It was [[Master Orrin Gilt]] in his nightshirt. He put his hand on the lock and prayed, and went back up. Nim locked it again and went to wake [[Ilyra Vessant|Ilyra]].

## Session 2 — The Office for Brother Matthias

*A purse lifted, a pick snapped, a bowl of soup, and a purse handed back.*

She walked into Master Gilt in the cloister and walked away with his purse, and with the very strong impression that the fat man was mostly coat. Fourteen gold pieces and a little pewter badge of the whole lamp. She kept it to herself until lunch, then told the others he was thin as a rake under all that.

She slept the afternoon. During the office she went down to the frosted door with [[Kael Stonehand|Kael]] while the whole house sang, put a pick in the lock, and snapped it off. The spare got her nowhere, and they ran when the singing stopped. At supper Gilt announced that somebody in this house was a thief. Nim ate her soup.

That night she pocketed her cup. After midnight, with the merchant gone and Lucan in his place, armour and all, she held out his purse. "I think you dropped this."

## Session 3 — Peace, Child

*First dagger, second dagger, one hit point, and the hat back. Again.*

She threw before anyone else moved and dropped [[Brother Hollis]], the cup man, over his register. Her second dagger dropped [[Brother Ansel]]. Then the abbot breathed and she was on one hit point and a prayer, and Grenna pulled her back.

She got her own snapped pick out of the crypt lock before Kael's key went in, because some things are a matter of pride. While Lucan was out she lifted his hat; when he woke she gave it back. "I think you dropped this." She wanted to wait for spring, and said so, and went down the chute anyway. The dragon offered her forty winters of coin and every name she owed, laid in the ice. She haggled. "Half. And the names first."

## Session 4 — Long Night Early

*Half the coin was a fair offer. The dragon said no.*

She put the first arrow past its head and kept putting them in. When it lifted its bleeding head and bargained, she offered it half the coin. It said no. "Then no."

On the coin, while the others were still breathing, she went through the pilgrims' packs. She found a coat with a valley stitch at the cuff and didn't make a joke; she gave it to Kael. She counted the hoard twice. "Tam's share goes to the valley." Nobody argued.

In [[The Chapel|the chapel]] at dawn she found [[Rosie Tallowby|Rosie]] with a knife and a bag of gold leaf. They settled on a third.
