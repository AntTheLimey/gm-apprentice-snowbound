## Session 1 — The Climb

*He brought them all up the pass. He could not bring Tam down.*

On the ice shelf the mule went over and the merchant shouted for his chest. Kael took the mule. The chest fell to a ledge; [[Nim Underbough|Nim]] went down for it. By noon cloud sat on the col and Kael chose the Widow's Stair. The merchant, [[Master Orrin Gilt]], named the left fork above the cairn before Kael could.

At dusk Kael saw the lamps of [[Saint Aubren's Rest]] through the cloud. In the guest wing [[Tam Rowe]] had his arms round him before he was through the door, asking about Hesta. Kael gave him her salve. At supper [[Brother Hollis]] brought Tam a cup to help him sleep, and Tam went to bed early.

In the morning his place was empty. Hollis said he had left before dawn; grief makes men foolish. There were no tracks: not on the path, not at the gate, not on the slope. Tam's pack was under his bed with the salve in it. Kael took the salve and went down the pass anyway. A hundred yards below the gate the snow was at his waist. Nobody had gone down that way. When he turned back, Hollis was holding the gate for him with a lamp.

## Session 2 — The Office for Brother Matthias

*Tam's name in the book, a cup on the floor, and an answer he didn't want.*

[[Brother Hollis]] let him read the register at the desk. Tam's name was there, in fresh ink, with a little lamp drawn beside it. "Guests we pray for, child. Every one." Kael closed the book.

He wanted the frosted door. In the afternoon [[Brother Teodor]] stood in the way. During the office he went back with [[Nim Underbough|Nim]], and the lock beat them, and they ran when the singing stopped.

That night he watched Hollis pour the merchant's cup from a little jug of its own, and knocked it out of the man's hand. "Never mind, child." After midnight the merchant became Lucan and named the house for what it was. Kael asked the only thing he had come up the mountain to ask. "Where's Tam?" "Below. With the others."

## Session 3 — Peace, Child

*He found Tam. He has not yet told Hesta.*

He put Hunter's Mark on [[Brother Teodor]], the man who had carried Tam down, and shot him twice, and Teodor fell. After the fight he took the crypt key off the body. Inside, by the chute, was [[Tam's Boot|Tam's boot]]. Hesta had stitched the cuff.

He chose the chute because it was the way they had carried Tam. Tam was at the bottom, in [[The Ice Cave|the ice]], in his shirt. Kael put the boot beside him and said something nobody heard. When the dragon offered him Tam's bones carried up whole, he said he would carry them himself. When it offered [[Grenna Fallowmere|Grenna]] three nights of breath for Hesta, and Grenna said she already had three nights in her coat, he turned and looked at her. She did not look back.

## Session 4 — Long Night Early

*He marked it for Tam. He finished it for Tam.*

He put Hunter's Mark on the thing that had eaten [[Tam Rowe|Tam]], and the first arrow went in. When it bargained, Grenna said four were blue. "Four?" Hesta was one, and Grenna had three shards in her coat. Tam had come up here to buy Hesta's life off them, he said, and look where it had put him. He nocked another arrow.

When it went under the ice with Lucan he came down the slope at a run with Tam's mark still on it and shot twice through the ice at the white shape. It let Lucan go ten feet short of the glacier wall. He lowered his bow and said nothing.

Nim gave him a coat with a valley stitch at the cuff. In the pocket was [[Hesta's Letter|Hesta's letter]], and he read it out. "She'd say don't pay for it. Give her them anyway." He took [[Boots of the Winterlands|the boots]] off a frozen pilgrim after Oswin had prayed over him, and carried Tam up the chute on the rope. He slipped halfway; Oswin took the other end. He laid Tam out in the chapel with the coat over him. At the first break in the weather he took Grenna down the pass, four days, a month early, and Hesta lived.
