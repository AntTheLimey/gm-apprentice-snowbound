### Backstory

I was left at the gate of Saint Aubren's Rest in a winter like this one, in a basket lined with a goat's fleece. Brother Hollis found me when he opened the gate for the morning, and the abbot named me Oswin and said the house would keep me. It has. Sister Wren taught me the open hand in the cloister yard, and Brother Teodor knocked me down every morning until I stopped falling. I copy the guest register fair for Brother Hollis each week, I carve the little wooden lamps we give the pilgrims, and in three years I take my vows. Novices do not go below the frosted door, and I have never asked to. The abbot says the Long Night vigil this year is to be mine.

### Appearance

Seven and a half feet of novice in a grey habit let out twice at the hem, barefoot on the cold flags by choice. Grey-blue goliath skin marked with darker whorls, a shaved head, and big careful hands that hold a lamp the way other people hold a bird. Moves quietly for his size: the brothers taught him.

## Notes

## GM Notes

Severin took Oswin in the winter after [[Master Orrin Gilt|Lucan]] ran away. [[Brother Ansel]] is the favourite, the pupil who stayed; Oswin is the one Severin was raising to carry. The Long Night vigil was to be his initiation: told the truth, given his vows early, and sent down with the first sleeper. Severin loves him and is counting on him; in the fight, Severin will not willingly harm him, and Teodor will pull his blows. Oswin has not been told anything, and none of the brothers has lied to him outright. They have only let him believe.
