## Session 3 — Peace, Child

*Moonlight on the abbot, three hands back off the floor, three shards in her coat.*

She came up the guest stair at the shouting and put a column of moonlight on the abbot, and it was her moonlight he died in. Between times she got Lucan up with a word, then Brannoc, then Nim. After the fight she got to Lucan first and kept him breathing, and saved [[Brother Hollis]] too, because that is what her hands do.

In the infirmary [[Sister Wren]] opened the reliquary for her: six shards of ice, and the same blue on the sick brothers' lips as on Hesta's. "Three nights, three shards." Grenna took three, wrapped them in a page of her case notes, and told nobody why. The cold on the way down got into her. Under the glacier the dragon named Hesta and offered her three nights of its breath. "I've already got three nights in my coat." It asked her who would carry it the one after. Kael was looking at her.

## Session 4 — Long Night Early

*Six shards, four sick. She did the sum out loud.*

The breath came at the lamp and she turned her face into the light and lived. Lucan didn't. She said a word and he got up, and the green closed Ilyra's frostbite. When the dragon offered to breathe for the sick every winter, she told it what was wrong with the offer: "Six shards is two cures. There are four of them blue." Then she told Kael what she had kept from him. "Hesta's one. I have three in my coat. I took them for her."

The second breath put her on one knee on nothing, and she refused to fall. The dragon took Lucan under the ice, and she said a word to herself, and a word under the ice, and he opened his eyes in its mouth.

On the coin Kael read Hesta's letter and looked at her. "Give her them anyway." "I was going to." At dawn [[Sister Wren|Wren]] asked for the shards back, and Grenna told her three were promised and Wren's were hers to choose. At the first break in the weather she went down the pass with Kael, a month early. Three shards over three nights, and the blue left [[Hesta Rowe|Hesta]]'s lips.
