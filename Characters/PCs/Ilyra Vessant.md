### Backstory

I have spent the better part of a century cataloguing the old mountain faiths: the saints who were gods first, the lamp rites, the carved thresholds. Most of it is dust and good handwriting, which suits me. A year ago Durgan Flintmere, a dwarf stonemason I trade letters with, sent me a rubbing of a lamp carving from Saint Aubren's Rest. Someone had chiselled half of it away, and what survives does not match any lamp rite I know. I wrote back that I would come and see the rest myself. Master Gilt was going up the pass for the winter and wanted a scholar who could also deal with trouble on the road. It was the first real invitation I had in years.

### Appearance

A tall, thin elf in a travelling robe with a dozen pockets, a grey cloak lined with wool, and spectacles on a chain. Silver-blonde hair tied back with a leather cord, ink on her left cuff, and a heavy spellbook in an oilcloth satchel that she checks as often as other travellers check their purses.

- **Traits:** Corrects mistakes aloud, then apologises. Sketches everything she cannot explain.
