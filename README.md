# Snowbound: a sample gm-apprentice campaign

A complete D&D 5e (2024) campaign vault, built and played end to end with
[gm-apprentice](https://github.com/AntTheLimey/gm-apprentice). Use it to see
what the skills produce, or to try them on a real campaign before you start
your own.

> Four sellswords are hired in Harrowford to escort Master Orrin Gilt, a fat
> and fussy cloth merchant, up the pass to Saint Aubren's Rest, a mountain
> monastery where he means to winter in prayer. The monks are the kindest
> people the party has ever met. The snow closes the pass behind them. Then
> the pilgrims start to disappear.

Chapter 1 is finished: four sessions, from the climb to the Long Night,
with every session's Plan, play notes and wrap-up. Its player site is live
at <https://snowbound-dnd.pages.dev>.

## What is in it

- `_Campaign/`: the Campaign Overview, timeline and picture guidance.
- `Chapters/Chapter 1/`: the chapter, its scene notes, and each session's
  Plan, play notes and wrap-up.
- `Characters/`: six PCs with full sheets and their stories, and the NPCs
  with their stat blocks.
- `Locations/`, `Creatures/`, `Items & Artifacts/`, `Factions & Organizations/`,
  `Clues/`, `Events/`, `Documents/` (the maps): the world, one note per thing.
- `_midwife/`: the working notes from the conversation that created the
  campaign.
- `_attachments/`: the campaign's pictures (portraits, scenes, maps).
- `_meta/`: the vault's settings and publish list. Publishing is off, and no
  site folder is set.

GM-only material (secrets, stat blocks, prep notes) is in the notes, as it
would be in your own vault. The player site keeps it out.

## How to use it

1. Install gm-apprentice in Claude Code (see its README).
2. Clone this repository and open the folder in Claude Code.
3. Ask for anything a GM would: prep Session 5, check the vault for
   contradictions, or publish the site. The first skill you run brings the
   vault up to your installed version.

To build its player site yourself, ask to publish the campaign. The publish
skill sets up a site folder and asks where to host it.
