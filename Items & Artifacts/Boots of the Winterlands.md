## Description

Fur-lined boots, taken off a frozen pilgrim in the dragon's cavern.

## What It Does

Boots of the Winterlands (uncommon, requires attunement; SRD 5.2): resistance to cold damage, ice and snow are no hindrance underfoot, and the wearer bears extreme cold.

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Kael took them off a frozen pilgrim after Oswin had prayed over him, and wore them down the pass with Grenna a month early.
