## Description

A small silver votive lamp, worth about 25 gp, worked with the whole lamp design, flame and all: the same as the wayside shrine's carving and [[Ilyra Vessant]]'s rubbing, without the part that has been chiselled away.

## History

Found in the offering niche of the wayside shrine on [[The Pass]], under the snow. [[Abbot Severin]] called it "an old ornament of the house" that the brothers set aside long ago.

## Related

- **Things:** [[The Lantern at the Pass]] — worked with its whole lamp symbol

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 01 - The Climb]]** — Found in the wayside shrine's offering niche; Ilyra kept it, and refused it to the abbot at supper.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Lucan asked for it; Ilyra kept it, lit it herself, and he said the prayer: the whole lamp burning. She carried it down to the dragon.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Ilyra held it up against the dragon's breath; the roof fell on it first.
