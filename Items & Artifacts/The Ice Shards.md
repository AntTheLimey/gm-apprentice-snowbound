## Description

Six shards of ice, cold as the glacier, kept in a locked reliquary in the infirmary. [[Sister Wren]] uses them on the sick brothers.

## What It Does

A shard pressed to someone heals them (2d4 + 3). Three, melted into a sick person over three nights, take the frost-blue from the lips: Wren says she has seen it every winter.

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Wren opened the reliquary for Grenna, who took three and told no one why. Three are left with Wren.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — The dragon said the sick have six between them. Grenna's three cured Hesta; Wren's three went to the younger of her sick brothers.
