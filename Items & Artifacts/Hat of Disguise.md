## Description

A merchant's fur hat, broad and too fine for the mountain.

## What It Does

Lets the wearer cast *Disguise Self* (SRD; uncommon, requires attunement).

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Gilt tore it off in the guest wing corridor, and the fat merchant was gone.
- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Nim lifted it off the unconscious Lucan and gave it back when he woke.

<!-- gm-only -->

## GM Notes

### Full Mechanics

*Disguise Self* lasts an hour, so the wearer renews it hourly. It fools the
eye, not the hand: a touch feels the real shape. The spell ends if the hat is
removed.

### Hidden Properties

[[Master Orrin Gilt]] wears it to look like the fat merchant. At the reveal he tears it
off. Afterwards it may become loot, or a clue the PCs find early.
