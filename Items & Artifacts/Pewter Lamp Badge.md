## Description

A small pewter badge of a lamp: the whole lamp, flame and all, the way it is carved over the abbot's door and on the wayside shrine.

## History

[[Nim Underbough]] found it in [[Master Orrin Gilt]]'s purse, with fourteen gold pieces, when she lifted the purse in the cloister. She handed purse and badge back to him at the unmasking.

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 02 - The Office for Brother Matthias]]** — Found by Nim in Gilt's purse; handed back at the unmasking.
