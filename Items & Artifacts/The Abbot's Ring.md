## Description

The abbot's ring of Saint Aubren's Rest: plain silver, cold to the touch. It opens every door in the house.

## What It Does

A Ring of Warmth (uncommon, requires attunement): when the wearer takes Cold damage, the ring reduces it by 2d8 (SRD). It also opens every lock in [[Saint Aubren's Rest]].

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 03 - Peace, Child]]** — Taken from the dead abbot; it opened his cell in the chapter house. Brannoc attuned to it in the short rest.
- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Took the cold out of the dragon's claw on Brannoc, and held against its second breath.
