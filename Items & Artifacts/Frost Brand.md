## Description

A frost-white longsword, found point-down in the ice of the dragon's hoard.

## What It Does

Frost Brand (longsword; very rare, requires attunement; SRD 5.2): extra cold damage on a hit, resistance to fire damage, light in freezing cold, and it can put out nearby flames.

## Campaign Log

- **[[Session 04 - Long Night Early]]** — Brannoc worked it out of the ice in the dragon's hoard.
